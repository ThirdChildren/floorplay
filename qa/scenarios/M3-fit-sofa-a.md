**The FitCheck is not `blocks-door`**: `blocks-door` (D16) is the 0.30 m free zone in front of a door and remains a reason for an invalid pose; the FitCheck uses the codes `door-too-narrow`, `door-too-low`, `fits-disassembled`, `no-route` and judges only the width and height of the doors on the route.
