Implementation prerequisites: T6.A6; for steps 4–5 T6.A7; for steps 2–3 and 6–7 T6.A8. Conventions: `qa/scenarios/M5-gate-notes.md` and `qa/scenarios/M5-budget.md` (K2, K3, K18, formula `callsPerView = calls / views`, source `[soglia:stats]` with a panel open, never `scene_get_render_stats`).

## Preconditions
- `BASE` = `house=apartment-a&role=tenant&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1&pinch=grip&furnish=scandinavian&time=2026-12-21T10:00` (K2: 14 pieces, menu closed).
- M5 standard setup for each step (file, `browser_reload_page`, `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode {mode: "hand"}`, wait 7 s, head (0; 1.6; 0), hands at rest).

## Steps
1. **Before the session**. Write `BASE`, reload **without** accepting the session. Log `pattern: "frames|stats|hud|log-tail"` → no `[soglia:frames]` line (there is no session); `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:debug-hud$` → 0 entities **or** 1 not visible (`Visibility` false); `xr_get_session_status` → `sessionActive: false`.
2. **Frame lines**. `xr_accept_session`, hands, wait 7 s. Log `pattern: "soglia:frames"`, at least 3 lines about ~2 s apart, each with **all** the fields `n`, `hz`, `avgMs`, `p95Ms`, `maxMs`, `over`, `fps`, `class`, `hzFrom` (at the end); `class` ∈ {`OK`, `LOW`, `FAIL`}; `hzFrom` ∈ {`session`, `est`} (in IWER `session`, `hz=90`); consistency: `p95Ms ≥ avgMs`, `maxMs ≥ p95Ms`, `fps ≈ 1000 / avgMs` (± 5 %), `n ≤ 120`. The `[soglia:stats]` and `[soglia:stats:groups]` lines still have **all the M5 fields** in the same order; the group `hud=<n>` is **at the end** of the groups line.
3. **In-headset panel**. Log `hud shown distance=<d>` with `d` in [0.55; 0.85] (measured by the engineer: **0.80**, about 36° from the line of sight, to the left and beyond the model; appears **only after** `xr_accept_session`). `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:debug-hud$` → 1 entity, `Visibility` true. `ecs_query_entity` `ui:debug-hud` with `["Transform"]` → distance from the head (0; 1.6; 0) in [0.55; 0.85] and distance from the center of the model (`miniature:root`) ≥ 0.50 m (it does not sit over the model). `ui_inspect` `ui:debug-hud`: the text contains `fps`, `ms`, `calls` and one of the words `OK`/`LOW`/`FAIL`; no text in Italian; no child outside the border. `browser_screenshot` → attach.
4. **Log tail** (T6.A7). Write `BH0&time=2026-02-30T10:00` (nonexistent date), where `BH0` = `BASE` **without** the `time=2026-12-21T10:00` (a `BASE` that already has `time=` does not work: in a URL or in a file **the first occurrence of the key wins**, so the nonexistent date would not even be read and there would be no `warn`: warning A6 of the 2026-11-07 gate), reload and accept the session. Log `pattern: "param time"` → **a single** line `param time="…" ignored: …` (warn); `ui_inspect` of the HUD: it contains `param time` (line truncated to ≤ 48 characters). No-doubling check: the `warn` line appears **once** in the log buffer (the hook forwards to the original, it does not duplicate it). Line `log-tail capture on lines=40` present once.
5. **Session summary**. Wait 12 s after the start of the session: log `pattern: "stats:summary"` → line with `seconds ≥ 10`, `minFps`, `worstMs`, `maxCallsPerView`, `maxTriangles`, `windowsBelow60`, `windows`; `minFps ≤ fps` of the last `frames` line; `maxCallsPerView ≥ callsPerView` read in `[soglia:stats]`. `xr_end_session` (CLI `xr exit`): a `[soglia:stats:summary]` line **at the end of the session** (also with `seconds` < 10, for example 14) and `hud hidden reason=no-session`; new `xr_accept_session`: the windows and the summary restart from zero (the first `frames` line has `n` ≈ 11 and the first `summary` `seconds=10`). The summary also comes out when the page goes to the background (`visibilitychange` hidden): **it is not proven in the emulator** — `F8` calls `applyVisibility` directly and **does not emit `visibilitychange`**, so it does not provoke that summary (warning A7 of the 2026-11-07 gate); it remains covered by the unit tests and by the headset test. The only accepted `warn` after `xr_end_session` is `THREE.WebGLRenderer: Can't change size while VR device is presenting.` (M5 contract).
6. **Cost in calls (K2)**. With `BASE` (HUD on), menu closed, at steady state (wait 8 s and after the first opening/closing of the menu to turn off the hint): `callsPerView_hud1` from the `[soglia:stats]` line and `hud_calls` from the `hud` group. Then `BASE&hud=0`, same state: `callsPerView_hud0`. **Checks**: `callsPerView_hud1 − callsPerView_hud0 ≤ 3` (measured by the engineer: **+2**, 52 against 50); `callsPerView_hud0` = K2 **remeasured at the gate under the same conditions** and written in the report (the engineer read 50; the 46 of `qa/scenarios/M5-budget.md` is the T5.RA forecast with other starting conditions, startup hint included: if the difference remains, compare with the "Measured" column of the M5 report, do not declare a FAIL a priori); with `hud=0`: `hud=0` in the group, 0 visible `ui:debug-hud` entities, log `hud hidden reason=hud0`, the `params` line ends with `hud=0`; with `hud=1` the `params` line does **not** have `hud=` at the end.
7. **Worst case (K18)**. K18 recipe of `M5-budget.md` (invalid `my-sofa` over `furniture:bed-double#1`, `pinned` menu open, reason and FitCheck labels, markers, role `tenant`) with the HUD on: `callsPerView` ≤ 100 (expected ≤ 75) and, with `hud=0`, **equal to K18 of M5 remeasured in the same state** (D65 rule below; the 2026-11-07 gate read 68 at steady state, 68 then 64 after release, 64 at rest, with HUD 70 / 70 then 66 / 66; tolerance ±1). Triangles ≤ 150,000 in both (read: 32,950 with `hud=0`, 34,006 with HUD).

   **D65 comparison rule (set by the 2026-11-07 gate, warning A2)**: "equal to M5" means **equal to M5 remeasured in the same state** (same house, same 14 pieces, `role=tenant`, `time=2026-12-21T10:00`, same way of opening the menu, same wait point), by building M5 `412790f` in a worktree and rerunning the same recipe; **never** against the numbers written in `M5-budget.md` (K2 46, K3 54/60, K12 23, K18 68/70/66/64), which cannot be reproduced even on M5. Gate numbers: M5 and M6 with `hud=0` **K2 50, K3 58, K12 26, K18 68**; with `hud=1` +2 (**52, 60, 28, 70**).
8. **Without `debug=1`**. `house=apartment-a&role=tenant&reset=1&seed=1&pinch=grip` (no `debug`): `xr_accept_session`, wait 7 s. No `[soglia:frames]`, `[soglia:stats…]`, `[soglia:env]`, `hud …`, `log-tail …` line; `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:debug-hud$` → 0; with explicit `hud=1` and without `debug`: a single `warn` `param hud ignored: needs debug=1`.
9. **High contrast**. `BASE&contrast=1`: `ui_inspect` of the HUD: background `#000000`, text `#ffffff` (the `theme-contrast` test guarantees it for the `.uikitml`).
10. Unfiltered log (`error`, `uncaught`, `Cannot read`, `reading 'add'`, `Missing glyph`, `budget exceeded`): no line beyond the intended `warn`s of step 4 and of `xr_end_session`/step 8; cleanup.

## Checks (all mandatory)
1. **Step 1**: no frame line and no visible panel before the session; the probe and the HUD do not start any session (`sessionActive: false`).
2. **Step 2**: three `[soglia:frames]` lines with all the fields and consistent; the M5 lines (`stats`, `stats:groups`) keep their fields, `hud` at the end.
3. **Step 3**: `ui:debug-hud` at 0.55–0.85 m from the head and ≥ 0.50 m from the model, text with `fps`, `ms`, `calls` and the status word, screenshot attached.
4. **Step 4** (T6.A7): the `warn` line appears in the HUD and **only once** in the logs.
5. **Step 5**: summary every ~10 s and at suspension, reset at a new session.
6. **Step 6**: HUD ≤ **+3** calls (gate: +2); `hud=0` = K2 of **M5 remeasured in the same state** (50).
7. **Step 7**: K18 ≤ 100 with HUD (gate: 70); equal to **remeasured M5** with `hud=0` (68).
8. **Step 8**: without `debug=1` no trace of the M6 tools (M5 unchanged).
9. **Step 9**: HUD in high contrast.
10. Console: 0 `error`; no `Missing glyph`, no `reading 'add'`, no `budget exceeded`.
11. **Headset only** (high): the real fps (`[soglia:frames]` with `hz` 72/90/120), the HUD can be read at 0.55–0.85 m without taking off the headset and without being annoying, the real cost of the UIKit panel, the `OK ≥ 70` threshold makes sense with respect to the refresh rate of the browser.

## Outcome
PASS if checks 1–10 are met (T6.A7 is delivered) · FAIL otherwise.
