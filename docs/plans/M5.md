**D54 · The sun: own algorithm, injectable clock** (T5.1, T5.2). No dependency (no SunCalc: BSD-2 licence, but 3 kB of code that we rewrite from public formulas and test, rule "no heavy dependencies"). `src/logic/sun.ts` implements the standard astronomical formulas (mean anomaly, ecliptic longitude, declination, right ascension, local sidereal time; the same as *Astronomical Algorithms* by Meeus and the NOAA calculator; **no code copied**, cited in a comment and in `CREDITS.md`, entry "Algorithms") and gives `sunPosition(utcMs, lat, lon)` → `{ azimuthDeg, altitudeDeg }` with the azimuth from north clockwise. Without atmospheric refraction (< 0.6° at the bottom, declared). **Time zone**: `time=` and the controls are the **local clock time of the house**; the demo houses are in Italy, so `euUtcOffsetMinutes(...)` applies the EU rule (UTC+1, UTC+2 from the last Sunday of March at 01:00 UTC to the last Sunday of October at 01:00 UTC); other time zones are **out of scope** (declared: "demo houses are in Italy"). The same `time=` stays, for the issue clock (D42), a UTC instant: it is declared in the log contract and creates no conflict (two uses of the same text, both deterministic). **State**: `AppState.sunTime: string` (local ISO `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`, minutes always `00`), saved and restored; **precedence**: explicit `time=` (URL or development file) > saved > default. **Default** without `time=` and without saved: the local time of the house **now** (injected clock, `Date.now` in production) rounded to the hour; if the sun is below the horizon, **10:00 of the same day** (so the first glance shows the light). Steps: hour ±1 h wrapping around 24; month ±1 with wrap (Dec → Jan) without changing the year, the day reduced to the maximum of the month. Reference values computed **by hand** on 2026-11-06 (to be confirmed with the NOAA calculator in the T5.1 test, tolerance ±1.5° in azimuth and ±1° in altitude): Turin 45.07° N 7.69° E, **21 Dec 10:00 local** (UTC+1) → azimuth ≈ 145°, altitude ≈ 13.7°; **21 Dec 15:00** → ≈ 216°, ≈ 13°; **21 Jun 14:00** (UTC+2) → ≈ 198°, ≈ 67.6°; **21 Jun 15:00** → ≈ 227°, ≈ 62°; **21 Dec 22:00** → altitude < 0.

**D55 · Light in the rooms without real shadows** (T5.3, T5.4). **Shadow-map shadows are cut**: a second rendering pass for the sun alone on a Quest costs more than the ~8–11 draw calls of the M4 forecast (which counted them separately) and forces R-A before everything else (−16 calls, ~4.5 real hours). In their place: **sun patches on the floor**, computed in pure geometry. For every window of an external wall: outward normal (perpendicular to the wall, from the side opposite the room that contains it); if the sun is at a horizontal angle θ from the normal with `cos θ > 0` and altitude ≥ `MIN_SUN_ALTITUDE_DEG` = 2°, the light that passes through the glass is projected on the floor (height 0) along `−sun direction`: the four corners of the opening (between `sill` and `sill + height`) become a parallelogram, clipped to the room polygon (Sutherland–Hodgman: requires **convex** rooms; a test verifies that the rooms with windows of the two houses are; otherwise the window is skipped with `swarn`). **Wall thickness**: grazing light is cut by the reveal: the useful width is `max(0, width − thickness × tan θ)` and the patch moves to the lit side; this explains why **at 15:00 the living room is in shadow** (θ ≈ 81°, reveal covering the whole glass) without an arbitrary threshold. Result per room: areas in m² (`litAreaM2`), used by the log and by QA. Rendering: **a single merged mesh** (`light:patches`, unlit material, transparent, `depthWrite` false, `polygonOffset`, slightly above the floor) = +1 call, and light anchors `light:<windowId>` (no mesh) for every lit window, for QA. It also works **at real scale** (the patches are children of the house). Declared limits: only the floor (no light climbing the walls), only the windows, no external obstacles; in the panel and in the Devpost it is "simplified light". It is not a shadow: for this reason the directional light (polish of T5.4, **to be verified** whether the materials are lit) does not cast shadows.

**D56 · "View" panel and mirrored button (T5.5, T5.6, T5.11)**: a **third plane of buttons** "View" (`ui:view-button-left/-right`) above "Issues", same mirrored layout and same anchor (centre at (∓0.4347; 1.635; −0.5665) at scale 0.05: 0.715 m and about 37.6° from the head; **design values, QA reads them from the `Transform`**; 1 cm gap between targets as for the others). The `ui:view-panel` panel is **a single root**, same mechanism and same `pinned` anchor with pitch as the other two (never together with the menu or the Issues panel, never with a piece in hand), same footprint as menu v2 (≤ 0.36 × 0.376 m; texts ≥ 2.4 cm). Content: **six uniform rows "label [−] value [+]"**: `Zoom` (scale: 60 %, 80 %, 100 %, 130 %, 160 %, 200 %, 240 % = 0.03/0.04/0.05/0.065/0.08/0.10/0.12), `Turn` (steps of 15°, turn 0–345°), `Month` (`Jan`…`Dec`), `Hour` (`10:00`), `Contrast` (`Off`/`On`), `Text` (`Normal`/`Large`); the header states the sun (`Sun 14° SE`, `Sun below the horizon`). Reason: **it reuses a single structure for everything** (row = three already known targets: light anchors + grab rectangles), keeps everything one-handed and gives accessibility and sun the same home. **T1.15 reduced**: one-hand zoom and rotation are these buttons (`miniature` changes with `setMiniature`, already existing; zoom limits 0.03–0.12 unchanged); the **one-hand drag on a room** proposed in M1 is **cut** (risk of confusing pan and selection, not testable in the emulator; the free zone of the base stays with `pan`, D28). If the budget plan requires it (T5.7), the three buttons on each side merge into one root (see "Risks").

**D57 · Agent: info points and Furnish / Clear** (T5.8–T5.10). **Info points are content, not state**: they are read every time from `public/demo/issues-<house>.json` (`kind: info` entries) in a read-only module, **not** in the store or in the saved state (no migration, `sortIssues` and the lists do not see them). The file for A goes from one to **five** `info` entries (boiler, washing machine, hob, electric shutter, double glazing; sample data in English, each with a `fixtureId` or a point), all by `agent`; in the app they appear with the label **"Sample data"**. New permission `view-info`: **`visitor` and `agent`**; the issue pins stay for `tenant` and `landlord`, so no screen has both. An info pin has a **different shape** (diamond-shaped head, second instanced geometry = +1 call) **and** a different colour (`0x20c5e8`), so it does not depend on colour alone (D46); a pinch within 2 cm of the head opens the Issues panel on the `info` screen (title `Info`, the text, "Sample data", no action). **Furnish / Clear**: in the `home` of the Issues panel for the `agent` role, `Furnish` and `Clear` appear (permission `stage`: `agent` only); `Furnish` exists only if the house has `staging` (A yes, B no: **no dead button**, in B only `Clear` remains). New **undoable** store action `stageFurniture(pieces, label)`: it writes a `stage` history entry (and `deserialize` accepts `stage`), so "Undo" goes back to the previous layout in one step (more useful than the `setFurniture` of `?furnish=`, which writes no history). The "Agent" chip **stays hidden** until T5.10 (carry-over 3: no "coming soon" in sight).

**D58 · High contrast and large text (T5.11–T5.13, T5.16)**. Preferences `prefs.contrast: boolean`, `prefs.largeText: boolean` (default false), saved; test parameters `contrast=1` and `text=large` (they count like `role=`: the explicit one wins over the saved). **High contrast**: interface set background `#000000`, text `#ffffff`, border `#ffd400` 0.4 cm (ratio ≥ **7:1**, verified by a test that computes the WCAG ratio of every pair used, ≥ 4.5:1 in the normal set); miniature with pure white walls, darkened floors, lighter door thresholds, pins with head +30 %. `theme.ts` exposes `getUiColors()` and `getPalette()` (no constant read at import): panels opened on demand (menu, Issues, View, labels) read the theme **at construction**, fixed buttons and materials are **recoloured in place** when the preference changes (UIKit `setProperties` and `material.color.set`: **to be verified in T5.12**; fallback: recreate the buttons with `disposePanelEntity`, never while a panel is opening). **Large text**: 3.0 cm (instead of 2.4) in the Issues and View panels, in the fixed buttons, in the Tabletop button, in the hint and in the labels (room, FitCheck, pattern, ruler). In the View panel with large text the row grows to 5.8 cm and the panel goes to 40 cm width (the cone test must stay ≤ 30°). **Menu v2 (palm and `pinned`) with large text** is T5.16 (**below the line**): 2 × 2 grid, bar on two rows, 4 pages instead of 3; if it is dropped, **the menu stays at 2.4 cm** and the log line `access text=large menu=normal` says so (declared in the Devpost).

**D59 · One-hand "Rotate"** (T5.14). For 12 s after a piece has been placed (and until another piece is picked up), next to the **placed piece** a small button `ui:rotate-button` appears (text `Rotate`, ≥ 2.4 cm, yaw-only, above the piece, never closer than 0.50 m to the head like the labels); a one-hand pinch claims it as `menu` and rotates that piece by +90° **in place** with `moveFurniture` (same placement rules: if the new rotation collides, the piece stays in the miniature with a red frame and the reason, as in T2.9b; the action is undoable). With a piece in hand it is not there (with one hand only the hand is busy: the wrist applies there). It does not replace the touch of the other hand nor the wrist.

**D60 · "Menu" button onboarding** (T5.15). After the onboarding pinch of M1 (`onboardingStep = done`), a **two-step coach** guides the minimum path: step `menu-button` (the ghost hand pinches the "Menu" button on the right side, with the text `Pinch Menu`) until the first opening of a menu (palm or button); step `grab` (the ghost hand carries the first piece into the house, text `Drop it in the house`) until the first placement; then `done`. Preference `prefs.coach: 'menu-button' | 'grab' | 'done'`, saved; whoever has already opened the menu (`menuOpened`) starts from `grab`. It also switches itself off with any pinch after 20 s. The hint `Palm up for the menu` (T2.16) stays. Texts in English, minimal.
