## Goal and scope

The house stops being something you only look at and furnish: whoever lives in it **reports a problem at the exact spot** (a long pinch on a fixture or on a point), the landlord sees it **in a list by urgency**, takes it on and resolves it, the tenant **confirms**, and everything stays in the **history**; the **move-in report** (blue pin) shows the defects that were already there. All with hands only, seated, and **everything saved automatically** (role and issues included): reloading the page or taking off the headset resumes from where you left off. This is act 3 ("Live") of the 3-minute demo and what moves the project from "furnishing game" to **Productivity**.

**Essential (carries the demo and the Productivity criterion)**: Block R prerequisites; role (visitor, tenant, landlord) with a switch from the panel and persistence; issue creation from a fixture with **preset phrases** and urgency; red/orange/green pins; list by urgency and detail with history; "Take on" → "Resolved" → "Confirm"; resume after reload; blue move-in report (from `issues-apartment-a.json`, "Sample data").
**Optional (below the cut line, cut first)**: voice note with `MediaRecorder` (T4.15, only if it exists and only if time is left), free text with the system keyboard (T4.16, headset only), viewpoint dead zone (T4.R6, fallback: smaller radius), W4 (T4.R7, cap of 30 minutes).
**Out of M4**: `Agent` (info points, map, questions: M5; the role already exists as a value and in the permissions table, the role button appears with M5), reporting at real scale (creation happens on the miniature), more houses, free comments, IndexedDB (only needed by the voice note).

**Rules** (`docs/RULES.md`): video under 3 minutes in three acts (choose, present, live): M4 provides act 3 (~50 s: long pinch on the washing machine → phrase → "Report" → red pin; landlord role → list → "Take on" → "Resolved"; tenant role → "Confirm"). *Best First Five Minutes*: no wall of text, a single new gesture (the long pinch) explained in one line in the panel. *Best Accessibility Forward*: everything with one hand (the two "Issues" buttons repeat the mirrored layout of the "Menu" buttons), "Tabletop" button at real scale (T4.R1), choice by pinch instead of typing. Rule 7: sample data (issues and move-in report loaded from the demo file) is labelled **"Sample data"**; roles are a demonstration selector, not accounts ("Demo roles, no accounts", rule 10).

## The problem of text with hands only

**Established fact**: in an immersive session the DOM is not usable (the project uses UIKit panels in the scene) and rule 1 forbids physical keyboards. Research done on 2026-11-02 (WebSearch; the Meta page could not be opened from the sandbox because of a network block, so **the search engine's summary of the official page `developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/web/webxr-keyboard/` applies, to be re-verified**):

| Option | What was found / what it costs | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Quest Browser system keyboard (WebXR) | Exists since Browser 26.1: `XRSession.isSystemKeyboardSupported`, you create an `<input>` in the DOM and call `focus()`, the text is read from `value`/`oninput`; while it is open the session goes to `visible-blurred` (the app is "suspended" from the point of view of our D30) and it closes with Done or by tapping outside. Cannot be tried in IWER; typing in the air with hands is slow and imprecise; needs an updated browser | **Optional**, behind feature detection, **headset only** (T4.16, to be cut first) |
| Virtual keyboard on a panel | ≥ 30 keys with text ≥ 2.4 cm and targets ≥ 3 cm → panel > 0.5 m wide: does not fit in the 30° cone at 0.5–0.8 m (rule 8); ~6 hours of work; typing errors with the pinch at 1:20 | **Discarded** |
| Dictation (`SpeechRecognition`) | A Meta forum thread (≈ 2025) reports that the Quest Browser does not expose it; on the PC (Chrome) it exists but would use a cloud service (rule 10, privacy) | **Discarded** (no credible fallback) |
| Free text in the 2D page, outside the session | Breaks the immersive flow and the demo | **Discarded** |
| **Preset phrases per category, chosen with the pinch** | Always works, hands only, one hand only, identical in the emulator and on the headset, fast (3 pinches for a report), localizable, testable, no browser API | **Recommended (D40)** |

**Recommended solution (D40)**: the category is proposed from the fixture (washing machine → `appliances`) or chosen (free point); for each category there are **three short phrases** plus "Needs attention"; the saved text is a full English sentence built from a template (`"The washing machine leaks."`). Urgency with three buttons (default Medium). No text field. The **voice note** and the **system keyboard** are feature-detected "extras": if the API is not there, the button **does not exist** (no dead buttons).
**Acceptance criteria in the emulator** (S4.1): full creation from a fixture in three pinches after the long pinch; creation from a free point in four; the saved text matches the expected phrase; no DOM element; no errors. **Uncertain, to be tried on the headset** (DEBT): reliability of the long pinch with real fingers; whether the system keyboard really appears and how our lifecycle reacts; whether the microphone is allowed in the Quest Browser.

## Design decisions

**Still valid**: D1–D38 (in particular D15/D28 arbitration, D18 `palm | pinned`, D29–D30 persistence and lifecycle, D32 buttons, D33 draw-call forecast), the user's gate rule of 2026-10-06 (only these block: errors in the console on the normal path, red tests/build, violations of `CLAUDE.md`, > 100 calls per view; everything else is a warning) and the schedule above.

**D39 · Scope and priority**: see "Goal and scope". Order of cuts inside M4: T4.15, T4.16, T4.R7, T4.R6 (fallback), then the "category" step of free points (T4.7), then the move-in report (T4.10).

**D40 · Text with hands only**: preset phrases (section above). Table in `src/ui/strings.ts` (`strings.issues.phrases[category]`: `{ id, label, predicate }`, `predicate` lowercase, e.g. `leaks`, `is blocked`, `does not start`). The saved phrase is `The ${name} ${predicate}.` with `name` = fixture name in lowercase (`washing machine`) or, for a free point, a name per category (`pipe`, `socket or switch`, `appliance`, `furniture`, `wall or floor`, `window or door`, `item`). Phrases (label → predicate): *plumbing* Leaks → `leaks`, Is blocked → `is blocked`, Low pressure → `has low pressure`; *electrical* Does not work → `does not work`, Buzzes or sparks → `buzzes or sparks`, Is loose → `is loose`; *appliances* Leaks → `leaks`, Does not start → `does not start`, Loud noise → `makes a loud noise`; *furniture* Is loose → `is loose`, Is broken → `is broken`, Is damaged → `is damaged`; *floors_walls* Is cracked → `is cracked`, Is damp → `is damp`, Is damaged → `is damaged`; *windows_doors* Does not close → `does not close`, Is stuck → `is stuck`, Lets in a draught → `lets in a draught`; *other* Is broken → `is broken`, Is noisy → `is noisy`, Is dirty → `is dirty`; in all: Needs attention → `needs attention`. Labels ≤ 22 characters (one line at 2.4 cm in the panel). The voice button (T4.15) and the note with the keyboard (T4.16) appear **only** if `typeof MediaRecorder !== 'undefined'` and `navigator.mediaDevices?.getUserMedia` exist (voice) / `session.isSystemKeyboardSupported === true` (keyboard); otherwise they do not exist.

**D41 · Role**: `AppState.role` (exists) gets the action `setRole(role)` (invalid value ignored). **Precedence**: an explicit `role=` in the URL or in the development file **wins** and overwrites the saved one; without `role=` the saved role is used; with nothing, `visitor`. `parseParams` exposes which keys were present (`present`, already exists): `mergeParams` returns them in `explicit`. The role is in the restore **whitelist** (D44). **The switch happens from the "Issues" panel** (D47: three chips Visitor / Tenant / Landlord at the top, `Agent` arrives with M5) and **not from the palm menu**: menu v2 is at its limit (36.0 × 37.6 cm, four tabs of 36.0 cm, R25 of M3) and a fifth element breaks the 30° cone or the texts ≥ 2.4 cm; the panel opens with a one-hand pinch from a side button (mirrored) next to the "Menu" button, so **it is reachable with one hand and from both sides**. Declared deviation from the request ("switch from the palm menu and from the pinned menu"): see question 2. Changing role closes an open draft (`issue draft cancelled reason=role`). Log `role changed from=visitor to=tenant source=ui`.

**D42 · Data model and anchoring**: `AppState.issues: readonly StoredIssue[]` with `StoredIssue = Issue & { sample?: true }` (`Issue` = an entry of `issue.schema.json`: `id`, `houseId`, `kind`, `position [x, y, z]` in **house coordinates** in metres, `fixtureId?`, `category`, `urgency`, `text`, `status?` (only for `kind: issue`), `history[]`); `sample` only in the store (the file does not have it: `additionalProperties: false`) and marks the entries loaded from the demo file. Other store fields: `nextIssue` (monotonic counter, starts at 14 because the file has `issue-010/012/013`), `issuesSeeded: boolean`, `prefs.moveInVisible: boolean` (D48). Cap `MAX_ISSUES = 40` (like the pieces). **Anchoring**: if the pinch falls within `FIXTURE_SNAP_WORLD_RADIUS` = 0.02 m of the world (0.40 m of floor plan at scale 0.05; 0.17 at 0.12) from a fixture, the report takes `fixtureId` and the **fixture's position** (the washing machine at (0.42; 0.85; 5.05)); otherwise the point is the **projection of the pinch onto the floor plan** (x, z rounded to 0.05 m) and the height `y = (y_pinch − y_floor) / scale` rounded to 0.05 m and limited to [0, `ceilingHeight`]; the point must be inside a room (polygon, margin 0.20 m towards the walls) otherwise no draft. `roomId` is **derived** (`roomAt`), not saved. `kind: baseline` entries are read from the file and saved like the others; `question` and `info` are not loaded in M4 (M5). **Clock**: `at` ISO with time zone: with `?time=2026-11-04T10:00` the clock is that instant **in UTC** and advances by 60 s at every event (`2026-11-04T10:00:00.000Z`, `…10:01:…`: deterministic for QA), without `time` it uses `Date.now()`. **Seed**: at the first start of a house (no saved state, `issuesSeeded` false) the `issue` and `baseline` entries are loaded from the file `public/demo/issues-<house>.json` with `sample: true`; if the file is missing or invalid: `swarn('issues seed unavailable …')` and the list starts empty (`issuesSeeded` stays false: it retries at the next start). The seed is not shown to visitors and agents (D46).

**D43 · State machine and permissions** (pure logic `src/logic/issue-state.ts`, `src/logic/permissions.ts`): `applyTransition(issue, action, role, nowIso, note?)` → `{ ok: true, issue } | { ok: false, reason }`. Actions and transitions (as in `docs/DATA_FORMATS.md`): `take-on` open → in_progress (landlord, event `in_progress`); `resolve` in_progress → resolved (landlord, event `resolved`); `confirm` resolved → archived (tenant, event `archived`); `reopen` resolved → in_progress (tenant, event `reopened`); every other pair is forbidden (`reason: 'invalid-transition' | 'role-not-allowed' | 'not-an-issue'`). Every transition adds a `history` entry `{ at, by, event, note? }` and does not mutate the original. Colours: open **red** `0xe5322d`, in_progress **orange** `0xf5a623`, resolved **green** `0x2ea44f`, archived **grey** `0x8a8f98` (hidden on the miniature, stays in the "History" list), baseline **blue** `0x2f7bf5`. Permissions:

| Action | visitor | agent | tenant | landlord |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| see pins and list | – | – (M5: info) | yes | yes |
| create an issue | – | – | yes | – |
| `take-on` / `resolve` | – | – | – | yes |
| `confirm` / `reopen` | – | – | yes | – |
| see the move-in report | – | – | yes | yes (read-only) |
| change role (demo) | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| `delete` an issue (only when it is `archived`; added 2026-11-17) | – | – | yes | yes |

**D44 · Full persistence**: same per-house key `soglia:v1:state:<house>`, **version 1 unchanged** (additive fields with defaults, like `view` in M3: `deserialize` of an M3 save works); no bump needed. The `pickRestorable` whitelist is extended to `role` (with the precedence of D41), `issues`, `nextIssue`, `issuesSeeded`, `prefs.moveInVisible`; a malformed `issue` is discarded on its own (`state issue discarded id=… reason=invalid`, warn) without losing the rest. `serializeForSave` also writes these fields. **Parked pieces** (carry-over 5): a saved piece with an unknown `catalogId` (for example `my-sofa` when `my-furniture.json` does not load) is **no longer discarded**: `pickRestorable` returns it in `parked`, the system keeps it aside and `serializeForSave(state, parked)` rewrites it after the store's pieces (cap `MAX_PIECES`); `unknown-room` is really discarded (the house has changed). Log `state piece parked id=… reason=unknown-catalog` (warn) and `parked=<n>` in `state saved`. Development parameter `failmy=1` (only with `debug=1`, like `failmodels`) simulates the missing own-pieces file, to try it. **IndexedDB: no** (40 issues ≈ 20 KB; it is only needed by a voice note, T4.15). Resume: the Issues panel and the drafts are **not** saved (interface state): on resume the pins are there, the panel is closed.

**D45 · Long pinch**: new owner of the `issue` pinch with priority between `pan` and `room`: **menu 8 > measure 7 > furniture 6 > viewpoint 5 > two-hands 4 > pan 3 > issue 2 > room 1** (update `CLAIM_PRIORITY` and the `pinch-claims` tests). Machine (pure, `src/logic/long-press.ts`): starts at `selectstart` if the role is `tenant`, the tool is `furnish`, we are not at real scale or in a transition, no piece is in hand, the hand is not claimed by others except `room`, and the point is inside the miniature's plane (`pinchToHouse` not null); stays `holding` while the hand moves less than `LONG_PINCH_MOVE_M` = 0.015 m (world) and lasts `LONG_PINCH_SECONDS` = **0.8 s** (a normal pinch lasts 0.1–0.4 s; the gestures that hold the pinch, grab/`pan`/two hands, start from different zones or take the hand before 0.8 s); at 0.8 s `claim(hand, 'issue')` (takes the hand away from `room`), deselects the room (`room deselected`), creates the **draft** and opens the panel at the `new` screen. If a second pinch arrives (two hands), a `selectend` or a movement beyond the threshold, the machine cancels (`issue hold cancelled reason=…`). For **all other roles** the long pinch does nothing (log `issue denied action=create role=<role>` once per pinch); on the empty base `pan` decides. Feedback: a **ghost pin** (instance of the pin, `pin:draft`) that grows from 0 to 1 in 0.8 s on the point (no extra call). Room selection already happens at `selectstart` (RayInteractable): it stays visible for 0.8 s and then disappears on creation (declared in the scenario, it is not a defect).

**D46 · Pins (`pin:*`)**: an **instanced mesh** (R-B, `createInstancedMarkers` extended to a merged "needle" geometry: stem + head, a single unlit material, colour per instance) with capacity `MAX_ISSUES + 2` (draft + selection ring) → **+1 call**; a light entity `pin:<issueId>` for every visible entry (Transform in the house, `StableId`, like the menu anchors) for QA and for the pinch. Constant size in the world (head Ø 1.6 cm, height 3.5 cm), the tip sits at `min(y, PIN_MAX_Y = 1.2 m)` of the house (walls are cut at 1 m: a fixture at 2.2 m does not fly above). **Visibility**: only `tenant` and `landlord` roles; `open`, `in_progress`, `resolved` on the miniature; `archived` no (only in the list); `baseline` only with `prefs.moveInVisible`; none at real scale. A pinch within `PIN_PICK_WORLD_RADIUS` = 0.02 m of a pin's head takes the hand as `menu` (maximum priority, like a button) and opens the detail; the nearest wins. The colour is **not the only sign** in the panel (status text in the list and in the detail); a shape sign on the miniature for colour blindness belongs to the accessibility work of M5.

**D47 · "Issues" panel**: **a single UIKit root** (`ui:issue-panel`), same footprint as menu v2 (≤ 0.36 × 0.376 m, texts ≥ 2.4 cm, so the cone and the thresholds of T4.R2 apply without recalculation), same anchor as the `pinned` menu (with the pitch of T4.R2), **never together with the menu** (opening one closes the other) and never with a piece in hand (like the buttons). It opens from two new **"Issues"** buttons (`ui:issue-button-left/-right`, above the "Menu" buttons, 0.07 m higher: (∓0.4347; 1.565; −0.5665) at scale 0.05, 0.715 m and 37.6° from the head, fallback entry allowed up to 45°; the text does not change; **+2 estimated calls**, to be measured). Screens: `home` (role chips; for `tenant`/`landlord` two buttons "Issues (n)" and "Move-in report"; for `visitor` a line explaining how to proceed; footer "Demo roles, no accounts"), `list`, `detail`, `new` (`category` step only for free points, then `what`), `movein`. Common bottom bar: `Back`, `Prev`, `Next`, `Close`. The controls are "light anchors + grab rectangles" like the menu items (same mechanism as `menu-items.ts`; if needed, a `panel-controls.ts` helper is extracted within T4.6a). The panel rebuilds itself **inside the same root** when the screen changes (no panel to create/dispose on the fly: the `PanelUISystem` trap stays confined to opening and closing, always with `disposePanelEntity`, and `flushPanelDisposals()` runs in its own system, T4.R3). A suspension (D30) closes the panel and discards the draft.

**D48 · List, detail, history, report**: `list`: 5 rows per page (`ui:issue-row-0…4`), order **by urgency**: first the active ones (`open`, `in_progress`), then `resolved`, then `archived` under the "History" heading; inside each group decreasing urgency (`urgent` > `medium` > `low`), then `open` before `in_progress`, then the most recent; row = status dot + text truncated to 24 characters + urgency; header with "Sample data" if the page contains `sample` entries. Expected order with the seed + a new `issue-014` (medium, open): **014, 013, 012, 010** (010 is `resolved` and stays at the bottom until the tenant confirms). `detail`: status, urgency, category, room, text (up to 3 lines), last 3 history entries (`28 Oct · landlord · Resolved`), action buttons according to D43 (**only** the allowed ones exist). `movein`: list of the `baseline` entries (one in the seed) and a "Show on model" switch (`prefs.moveInVisible`, saved); the blue pin is seen on the miniature only with the switch on. Everything is read-only for the landlord.

**D49 · Carry-overs: minimal solutions**:

- **R2 · `pinned` menu with a tilted gaze**: `pinnedMenuAnchor(head, yaw, pitch)` compensates the gaze pitch at opening **within a limit**: `c = clamp(pitch, −8°, +8°)`, the height becomes `head.y − 0.20 + 0.55·tan(c)` (panel always vertical and yaw only). The pitch window to stay in the cone widens from **+5.1° / −7.1°** (12.2°) to **+12.0° / −14.3°** (26.4°; the +13.1° / −15.1° of the first draft added 8° to both limits without recalculating with the real menu measurements, corrected by T4.R2); beyond −14.3° the panel goes out by a few degrees (warning, the gaze is not chased so as not to cover the miniature). The same anchor serves the Issues panel. Line `menu pinned pitchDeg=<g> anchorPitchDeg=<c>`.
- **R3 · label and pillar**: the room label is drawn **above the markers** (same treatment as the pattern label: high render order and no depth test); fallback: hide the marker within 0.06 m for the duration of the label. In addition (carry-over 6) `placeRoomLabel` never goes below `floor + wall + 0.047 m`.
- **R4 · viewpoint dead zone (deferrable)**: the pinch on a marker **does not enter immediately**: `viewpoint` takes the hand as today (so it does not select rooms or move the miniature) but **confirms after 0.30 s** of still pinch (≤ 0.02 m) with the other hand not pinching; if the second pinch arrives earlier, the confirmation is cancelled and the two-hand gesture starts (zoom near a marker). The marker pulses during the wait. Fallback (0.2 h): grab radius 0.04 → 0.025 m. Updates S3.1 (pinch duration ≥ 0.6 s on the markers).
- **R5 · own pieces**: D44 (parked). **R6 / R9 / scenarios**: see Block R. **R8 (`README`, `DATA_FORMATS`)**: in M7.
