## What blocked and how it was closed

**F-S3.5-1** (rule: console error in the normal path): `[PanelUISystem] Failed to load panel for entity N: TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'add')`, reproduced 2 out of 2 times by carrying the wheelchair from the `fit` tab toward the bathroom without releasing it. Cause: `PanelUISystem.loadPanel` is asynchronous and a panel disposed before its `PanelDocument` arrives loses its `object3D`; `fit-label-panel.ts`, `reason-label-panel.ts` and `menu-hint-panel.ts` called `entity.dispose()` directly. Fix: `disposePanelEntity` (already used for the same defect in M2) in the three panels. Rerun: 27 trials, ~45 console `error`/`warn` reads all empty, S3.5 PASS, 51–67 calls per view. The only case not proven is the race of the menu hint, which the emulator cannot trigger (debt for the headset).
