# Video script — Floorplay (target 2:40, hard limit < 3:00)

Rules that shape this script (from the official rules):
- Under 3 minutes; judges are not required to watch beyond 3:00.
- Footage "as viewed on a Meta Quest device or via XR Simulator": record **in the headset** (Quest screen
  recording or casting). Use emulator footage only as a fallback for a shot that fails on the headset.
- "Show real gameplay that honestly represents the experience. Lead with the story." No AI-generated video.
- English voice-over **and** burned-in English subtitles (also helps viewers with the sound off).
- No logos, brands or recognizable people other than the entrant. Do not show the Meta logo or imply endorsement.
  Your own hands in passthrough are fine; avoid other people and branded objects in the room.

Recording setup: Quest screen recording (Settings → Camera → Record video) or cast to a PC and capture at
1080p 30/60 fps. Seated at a table. Start each take with `?reset=1` so the home is clean. Turn on
`text=large` only if small text is unreadable in the recording. Keep head movements slow.

## Shot list for capture (in this order, one home, `?reset=1`)

1. Enter VR from the start screen (do 2–3 takes; keep the one where the model appears cleanly).
2. Two-hand zoom/turn, pinch a room label.
3. Menu → sofa → drop; bed over a door (red frame, "Blocks the door"); Rotate; palm-up menu.
4. Blue marker → real scale → row-walk into the hall → Tabletop.
5. View panel: Month and Hour changes with the light patches.
6. New take with `?role=agent&reset=1`: info diamond, Furnish, Undo.
7. New take with `?reset=1`: Issues → Tenant → hold pinch on the washer → form → Landlord → orange → green.
8. Headset off/on (resume). View → Contrast, Text: Large, Hand.
9. Final wide shot.

## Honesty notes

- Say nothing about fps numbers unless measured on the Quest.
- Keep the "Sample data" labels visible when showing info points, the issue history or the move-in report.
- Mixed reality (`?mr=1`) is experimental: leave it out of the video, or show it only with the caption
  "Experimental: mixed reality".
- Do not speed up footage to hide waits; cut instead.
