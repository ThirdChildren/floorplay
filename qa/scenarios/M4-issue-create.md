## M4 conventions (valid for all `M4-*` scenarios)
- `pinch=grip`: `D_GRIP` = (−0.038; −0.003; +0.037); hand pose to hit `T` = `T − D_GRIP` = (Tx + 0.038; Ty + 0.003; Tz − 0.037), orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)`. Head (0; 1.6; 0); `O` = (0; 1.35; −0.45); scale 0.05; `W(px, pz, y)` = (Ox + (px − 5.5)·0.05; Oy + y·0.05; Oz + (pz − 3.6)·0.05) with `y` in **house** meters (a pinch at 0.04 m from the plane = 0.8 m of house).
- **Long pinch** = `xr_set_select_value` to 1 on the hand, **hand still**, wait 1.5 s (the threshold is 0.8 s; every MCP call costs 0.5–3 s), then `xr_set_select_value` to 0. **Never** `xr_select` with `duration` < 1 s for the long pinch. Short pinch = `xr_select {duration: 0.3}`.
- **Panel controls and buttons**: they are read with `ecs_query_entity` `["Transform"]` at every screen (the positions change) and pressed with the right hand at `position − D_GRIP`, `xr_select {duration: 0.3}`. Button `ui:issue-button-right` at (0.4347; 1.565; −0.5665) → pose (0.4727; 1.568; −0.6035); `ui:issue-button-left` at (−0.4347; …) → (−0.3967; 1.568; −0.6035); `ui:menu-button-right` → (0.4727; 1.498; −0.6035).
- **Fixtures** (world, scale 0.05): `fixture:washer` (0.42; 0.85; 5.05) → (−0.254; 1.3925; −0.3775); `fixture:boiler` (2.3; 1.6; 4.8) → (−0.160; 1.430; −0.390); `fixture:kitchen-unit` (0.4; 0.9; 2.2) → (−0.255; 1.395; −0.520); `fixture:bedroom-shutter` (6.8; 2.2; 0.1) → (0.065; 1.460; −0.625). **Free** points of the scenario: `FREE_STUDY` = plan (9.6; 3.9), y = 0.8 m → (0.205; 1.39; −0.435) (at 0.10 m from the marker `viewpoint:V3` (0.215; −0.535), outside its radius of 0.04 m); markers `viewpoint:V1/V2/V3` at (−0.130; −0.465), (0.065; −0.470), (0.215; −0.535).
- **Clock**: `time=2026-11-04T10:00` sets the issues clock to `2026-11-04T10:00:00.000Z` in UTC and advances it by 60 s at every event (D42).
- Standard setup: write `dev-params.local.txt`, `browser_reload_page`, `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode {mode: "hand"}`, wait 2.5 s; hands at rest `(∓0.30; 1.10; 0.10)`. A load **without** `reset=1` inherits the saved state. The `[soglia:state]` log now carries the issues in compact form (id, type, status, urgency); the texts and history are read from the lines `issue created` and `issue history`. Read with `since` and narrow filters: `[soglia:hands]` and `[soglia:stats]` fill the buffer.
