## Steps
1. **Buttons**: `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:menu-button-` (→ 2) and with `^ui:palm-menu$` (→ 0); `ecs_query_entity` on each with `["Transform"]` (→ `BL`, `BR`); log `pattern: "menu button|menu view"`; `ui_inspect` on the button (text `Menu`, `fontSize`); `browser_screenshot` (head still, `xr_look_at` `headset` toward (0; 1.35; −0.45)) → attach.
2. **Opening with one hand**: `hand-right` at `BR − D_GRIP`, wait 0.3 s, `xr_select {device: "hand-right", duration: 0.2}`, wait 0.8 s. Readings: log `pattern: "menu|pinch"`, `count: 20`; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:palm-menu$` and `^ui:menu-`; `ecs_query_entity` on `ui:palm-menu` with `["Transform"]`. The hand returns to rest `(0.30; 1.10; 0.10)`; wait 3 s; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:palm-menu$`; log `pattern: "menu"`.
3. **Use it with the same hand**: (a) page: `xr_select` on `ui:menu-page-next` (hand at `item − D_GRIP`, read with `Transform`); (b) grab: `ui:menu-item-desk` (now the page is 2/3), `xr_set_select_value` 1 + 0.4 s, `xr_animate_to` to P1 (0.8 s), release (`furniture placed … status=valid`); (c) Undo: `xr_select` on `ui:menu-undo` → `undo action=place`; (d) Recenter: move the miniature with the right-hand `pan` (hand at `B1`, select 1, 0.4 s, `xr_animate_to` to (0.288; 1.383; −0.487), select 0: `miniature translated x=−0.100…`), then `xr_select` on `ui:menu-recenter` → `miniature recentered scale=0.0500`, `miniature:root` returns to (0; 1.35; −0.45) ± 0.01. After each action: `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:palm-menu$` and log `pattern: "menu|furniture|undo|miniature|pan|pinch left"`.
4. **Closing**: the right hand on the button `BR` (re-read the `Transform`), `xr_select 0.2`, wait 0.8 s: log, `^ui:palm-menu$`. Then **reopening with the other button and closing with the first**: `hand-left` at `BL − D_GRIP`, `xr_select {device: "hand-left", duration: 0.2}` (here the left moves: it is the only step that uses it, to test the mirror) → `menu opened hand=left mode=pinned`; then `hand-left` at rest and the right on `BR`: closes.
5. **Replacing the palm menu**: `hand-left` at `L_MENU` with `Q_UP`, wait 0.9 s (`menu opened hand=left mode=palm`); `hand-right` on `BR`, `xr_select 0.2`: log; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:palm-menu$` (must be **1**). With the `pinned` menu open and the left still up: wait 1.5 s, still 1 entity. Palm down (left): the `pinned` menu **stays**. Close with the button.
6. **Piece in hand**: `pinned` menu closed; the right takes `chair` from the palm menu (left up, `ui:menu-item-chair`, grip correction) and **holds**; the left (palm down) goes to `BL − D_GRIP` and `xr_select {device: "hand-left", duration: 0.2}`: log `pattern: "menu button|menu|furniture rotated"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:palm-menu$`. Release outside the house (POUT1).
7. **F-A, palm menu (as S2.1 step 9f)**: start from menu closed and hands at rest; `hand-left` at `L_MENU` with `Q_UP`, wait 0.9 s (`menu opened hand=left mode=palm`); **without moving the hand**, `xr_set_select_value` `hand-left` = 1, wait 1.5 s; then = 0, wait 1.5 s. Log `pattern: "pinch|room|menu|onboarding"` (with `since`); `[soglia:state]` (`selectedRoomId`). Then palm down. Repeat with the **right** (`R_MENU`, `Q_UP`).
8. **F-A, the room pinch stays valid**: `pinned` menu open (step 2), `hand-right` at (Cx; 1.6; Cz + 0.02) with `xr_look_at` toward (Cx; 1.35; Cz) for the living room (Cx = −0.145, Cz = −0.515), `xr_select {device: "hand-right", duration: 0.3}`: log `pattern: "room|menu"`. Then the rotation pinch: with the palm menu opened by the left and a piece in the right hand, the pinch of the **left** (menu hand) rotates the piece (`furniture rotated … rot=90`) and does not select the room.
9. Unfiltered log (`error`, `Cannot read`, `Failed to load`, `Missing glyph`); cleanup as in S3.0.
