# S2.3 · Collision between furniture: red outline and return to valid

**Milestone**: M2 · **Type**: PC (IWER emulator via MCP `iwsdk-runtime`)
Implementation prerequisites: all those of S2.2 (`qa/scenarios/M2-place-bed.md`) plus T2.4 (collisions), grabbing a piece already in the miniature (T2.13) and **T2.9b** (reason label, check 10). Decisions D14, D16, D27 of `docs/plans/M2.md`: an invalid piece **stays in the miniature** with the red outline and a label that explains the reason.

## Preconditions
- As S2.2: `dev-params.local.txt` = `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1`, reload, accept, hands, head (0; 1.6; 0), O from the last `miniature placed` line (expected (0; 1.35; −0.45)), menu open with `hand-left` at `L_MENU` and `Q_UP` and **held up for the whole scenario**. `W(px, pz) = (Ox + (px − 5.5)·0.05; Oy + 0.04; Oz + (pz − 3.6)·0.05)`.
- Points (O = (0; 1.35; −0.45), y = 1.39):
  - **PW** (wardrobe) = `W(7.4; 0.55)` = (0.095; 1.39; −0.6025) → expected snapped to (7.44; 0.425) in the north-east corner of the bedroom.
  - **PB1** (bed, valid pose) = `W(6.0; 3.0)` = (0.025; 1.39; −0.480) → expected (6.06; 3.00) flush with the west wall of the bedroom.
  - **PB2** (bed, on the wardrobe) = `W(7.0; 1.2)` = (0.075; 1.39; −0.570) → expected (7.00; 1.125), overlapping the wardrobe.
- **Tolerance on positions**: the hand poses are set by hand, and IWER shifts the pinch point by about 1.2 cm while the pinch closes; the gate report (`qa/reports/M2-2026-10-06.md`, F3) measured differences of 0.05 m of plan, with no fixed offset (error < 0.0002 m of plan from four opposite directions, no sign pattern). For the final positions of the pieces **±0.10 m of plan applies** (no longer ±0.02). The **exact precision** of the snap to the walls and of the 0.05 m grid is **not checked here**: it is covered by the unit tests `tests/unit/placement-rules.test.ts` (snap, grid, idempotence), `tests/unit/furniture-grab.test.ts` and `tests/unit/furniture-pose.test.ts` (hand → plan), which run with `npm test`. What counts here are the **states** (`valid`/`invalid`, reason, outline, label), not the centimeter.
- The `wardrobe` (page 2) and `bed-double` (page 1) entries are reached with `ui:menu-page-next` / `ui:menu-page-prev` (short pinch `xr_select {device: "hand-right", duration: 0.2}` on their position).

## Steps
1. Menu open. `ui:menu-page-next` → page 2. **Wardrobe**: `ecs_query_entity` on `ui:menu-item-wardrobe` (Transform) → `I`; `hand-right` at `I` (orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)`), `xr_set_select_value` 1, wait 0.3 s, `xr_animate_to` to **PW** (0.8 s), wait 1 s, `xr_set_select_value` 0, wait 0.5 s. Log `pattern: "furniture"` and `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:wardrobe#1` with `["Furniture"]`.
2. `ui:menu-page-prev` → page 1. **Bed in valid pose**: same procedure with `ui:menu-item-bed-double` up to **PB1**. Log and `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:bed-double#1`.
3. **Push the bed against the wardrobe**: `hand-right` at the center of the bed, `W(6.06; 3.0)` = (0.028; 1.39; −0.480), orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)`, select 1, wait 0.3 s (log `furniture grabbed … source=model`). `xr_animate_to` to **PB2** (0.8 s), wait 1 s. **In flight**: `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:bed-double#1` with `["Furniture"]`; log `pattern: "furniture status|reason"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^ui:reason-"` and `ecs_query_entity` on the label found with `["Transform"]`. `browser_screenshot` (red outline **and reason label**).
4. Release (`xr_set_select_value` `hand-right` = 0, wait 0.5 s). Log `pattern: "furniture placed|furniture status|reason"`; `ecs_query_entity` with `["Furniture"]` on the bed and on the wardrobe; latest `[soglia:state]` line; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:reason-`. `browser_screenshot` (bed resting with persistent red outline and reason label still visible).
5. **Move it → valid**: repeat the grab of the bed (current center = `W(x; z)` with `x`, `z` from the `furniture placed` line of step 4; expected about `W(7.0; 1.125)` = (0.075; 1.39; −0.574)), `xr_animate_to` to **PB1**, release. Log `pattern: "furniture placed|furniture status|reason"`; `ecs_query_entity` on the bed; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:reason-`.
6. Final check: `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^furniture:"` and `"^ui:reason-"`; `browser_get_console_logs` without a filter (look for `error`).

The wardrobe stays `valid`.
5. Step 5: `furniture status furniture:bed-double#1 status=valid reasons=-` and `furniture placed … x=6.06 z=3.00 … status=valid` (x and z within ± 0.10 of 6.06 and 3.00); `Furniture.status` = `valid`, `outline` = `none`.

7. No `room selected`/`room deselected` line, no `miniature gesture start`; scale 0.05 and yaw 0 unchanged in the store.
8. No `error` entry.
9. Screenshots of steps 3 and 4 attached; the report says in words that the outline is **red** when the bed overlaps the wardrobe and that it disappears when the bed returns to a valid pose.
10. **Readable reason (D27, T2.9b)**:
    a. Step 3 (in flight): **1** `ui:reason-bed-double#1` entity exists and the line `[soglia] reason shown furniture:bed-double#1 "Overlaps the wardrobe"` (**exact** text, in English, lowercase name from the catalog); its position is **0.52 m or more from the head** (`LABEL_DISTANCE_MARGIN`; Transform rounding tolerance 0.001) and **≤ 0.65 m**, and at `y ≥ Oy + 0.04` (above the piece); the label is **inside the central 30° cone**: with the head at (0; 1.6; 0) and facing (0, 0, −1), `acos(−dz / |d|)` with `d` = position − (0; 1.6; 0), computed from the `Transform` of step 3, is **≤ 30°**; it is **yaw-only** (rotates only around the vertical axis, rotation with x ≈ 0 and z ≈ 0); the screenshot shows the label near the bed, **fully readable** (all the text "Overlaps the wardrobe" visible: not cut off by the edge of the view, not covered by the dimmed menu or by buttons), **with no Italian text**. A distance of 0.515 m or an angle of 30.5° are **warnings** (user gate rule), while cut-off or unreadable text is a FAIL of rule 8 of `CLAUDE.md`.
    b. Step 4 (after the release): the label, if the bed changed place, still has distance ≥ 0.52 m from the head and angle ≤ 30° (the `Transform` is read again after the release); the label stays (**1** `ui:reason-bed-double#1` entity, no new `reason hidden` line) as long as the piece is `invalid`; the wardrobe, valid, has none (`ui:reason-wardrobe#1` absent); the reasons in the state log are only `overlaps-furniture` (the text never says "wall").
    c. Step 5 (valid again): **0** `ui:reason-*` entities and the line `[soglia] reason hidden furniture:bed-double#1`.
    d. Step 6: **0** `ui:reason-*` entities at the end; never more than **3** simultaneous labels in the whole scenario (here at most 1 exists).

## Outcome
PASS if checks 1–8 and 10 are met · FAIL otherwise. If the grab of the already placed bed does not start, repeat **only once** with the hand at ≤ 0.02 m from the center and report the difference.
