# UI fixes after the first headset test (2026-11-14)

Branch `claude/ui-fixes`. Four changes asked by the owner after trying the app on the Quest. Emulator (IWER, headless, software
rendering) only: nothing here has been tried on a headset (see `qa/device/DEBT.md`, rows `UI-FIX`).

## What changed

### 1. Menu buttons on one hand, panels close to the head
- The fixed `Menu`, `Issues` and `View` buttons (the "palm menu actions") are on the RIGHT of the model by default. The buttons of the
  other side are hidden and cannot be pinched. The coach points at the active button.
- New saved preference `prefs.leftHandMenu` (additive: an older saved state reads as `false`). The switch is the new `Hand` row of the
  View panel, next to `Contrast` and `Text` (the accessibility options): `L` picks the left hand, `R` the right hand, a press on the value
  flips it. Log lines `view step row=hand value=left`, `access hand=left source=ui`. The label is `Hand` (values `Left` / `Right`) because
  the longer `Menu on left hand` does not fit the 11 cm label cell at 2.4 cm text (21 cm wide).
- The View panel has seven rows now, so it is 41.2 cm tall (45.1 cm at large text) instead of 37.6.
- The menu, the View panel and the Issues panel open about 0.52 m in front of the head (large text 0.6 m), the middle of the panel 3 cm
  below the eyes, facing the user, following the pitch of the gaze at the opening (-20 to +8 degrees; before it was clamped to
  +-8, so a user looking down at the model found the panel at the horizon, at the edge of the view). Whatever the pitch the whole panel
  is slid inside the 30 degree view cone (`pinnedPanelAnchor`, `src/logic/menu-anchor.ts`). The room-view buttons keep the +8 limit
  so they never touch the menu.
- The coach banner "Drop it in the house" hangs under the open menu (it used to land on its cards now that the menu is in the cone).

### 2. Door-fit messages removed
FitCheck system, label panel and layout, door markers, `door-graph`, `fit-check`, `fit-label`, `fit-markers`, the `Fit` menu tab
(wheelchair, stroller), the fit colours, `Furniture.fit/fitDoor`, the strings and their tests. The catalog data format is unchanged
(`kind: "mobility"` and `disassemblable` are still valid; the two mobility items stay in `catalog.json` but are not offered any more).
The placement reasons (`Blocks the door`, `Overlaps ...`) are a different feature and stay. The FitCheck scenarios and docs are marked
removed; reports are untouched.

### 3. Colours and buttons that do not overlap
- Two button roles in the theme (`src/ui/theme.ts`): primary (blue, white text: `Menu`, `Close`, the add button of the scan card,
  `Tabletop`) and secondary (pale blue-grey: every other button, chip, tab). Selected chips stay dark (yellow in high contrast).
  High contrast: primary is cyan with black text, secondary is dark grey with the yellow border. WCAG ratios are tested.
- Overlap found and fixed: the icon of the menu bar buttons (`Undo`, `Back`, `Next`, `Recenter`) overlapped their labels (the icon drawn
  at 2.4 cm in a 2.4 cm slot left no room); the slot is 1.4 cm now with a 0.6 gap, a test keeps icon + gap + label inside the button.
- The cells of a View panel row and the bar (note / `Close`) have wider gaps. The two menu tabs share the row.
- Screenshots (20, runtime screenshots inside an XR session, headless IWER): `qa/reports/UI-FIX-<normal|contrast|large|large-contrast>-<buttons|menu-in-view|menu|view|issues>.png`.
  `-buttons` and `-menu-in-view` are what the user sees from the seat (800 x 800); the others are taken with the head 0.3-0.36 m from the panel.

### 4. Tape measure removed
Measure system, label, hint, visuals, pure logic, the `Measure` tab, `AppState.tool` and `setTool`, the `measure` pinch owner, the
`measureSnap` knob (25 knobs left), the amber / tape colours and their tests. An older saved state (which never stored `tool`, but may
carry other keys) still loads: stale keys are ignored (`tests/unit/removed-features.test.ts`). M-scenarios and docs marked removed.

## Checks
- `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` (145 files, 6.7k tests), `npm run build`: green on every commit.
- Emulator: right-hand buttons only; `L` in the View panel moves them to the left (`access hand=left source=ui`), the left Menu button
  opens the menu with the left hand (`menu opened hand=left mode=pinned`); no console `error` or `warn` after a fresh `dev up`, through
  menu / View / Issues in the four settings. Frame distance logged at a gaze of -29 degrees (the model, the worst case): menu 0.68 m, View 0.71 m,
  Issues 0.68 m (large text 0.78-0.83 m), `maxAngleDeg=30.0` (the cone limit, as the slide leaves it).

## Verified only in the emulator / open points
- Everything above. Nothing was tried on a headset: hand reach, panel comfort, real colours: `qa/device/DEBT.md` rows `UI-FIX`.
- Rule 8 deviation at large text with a gaze steeper than -20 degrees: the corners of the large panels reach 0.84-0.87 m (the rule says 0.8 m;
  normal text stays between 0.52 and 0.76 m). Tested and documented in `tests/unit/panel-anchor.test.ts`.
- The tuning knobs `pinnedDistance` / `pinnedDrop` now move only the room-view panels (the three panels use the new constants
  `PANEL_DISTANCE`, `PANEL_DROP`, `PANEL_PITCH_*` of `src/logic/menu-thresholds.ts`).
- `CLAUDE.md` rule 9 was NOT changed: the request reached me through an agent message, which cannot authorise an edit of `CLAUDE.md`.
  Proposed wording for the owner to apply: "9. **Accessibility.** Everything usable with one hand only (palm menu on the right hand
  by default, movable to the left in the accessibility settings), high-contrast theme, large text."
  The same file still lists `FitCheck` and `Measure` in its architecture table and in the project summary.
- Not done: the Issues panel's form / detail / move-in screens were not re-inspected one by one for overlaps (home and list were);
  the room-view (`?mr=1`) panels were not restyled beyond the shared button colours.
