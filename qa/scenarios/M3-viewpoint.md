2. **Entry**: right hand at `M1 − D_GRIP`, `xr_select {device: "hand-right", duration: 0.3}`, wait 2.5 s (the fade lasts < 0.7 s; MCP latency hides it). Readings: log `pattern: "viewpoint|walls|fade|miniature|room selected|gesture"`, `count: 20`; `ecs_query_entity` on `miniature:root` with `["Transform"]` (→ `R1`); `xr_get_transform` `headset` (→ `H1`); `ecs_find_entities` with `^wall:`, `^door:`, `^window:`, `^viewpoint:`, `^ui:fade-overlay`, `^table:plinth`; `browser_screenshot` (head still, `xr_look_at` `headset` toward (0; 1.6; −1.5) to look at the living room) → attach.
