## Rules the scenario checks
- **One hand (`pan`)**: pinch on the **free base** (`table:plinth` disc, outside every room and every piece, beyond `PAN_GUARD` = 0.45 m real from the room polygons) and drag: the center follows the horizontal movement of the hand.
- **Two hands**: the midpoint is the pivot (formula of D28).
- **Limit**: center within **0.30 m** of the anchor, horizontally (projected onto the circle), **height (y) never changed**, no tilt.
- **Pinch priority** (D15): menu > furniture piece > two hands > `pan` > room. A pinch on a piece or on a room does **not** move the miniature; a pinch on the base does **not** grab pieces or select rooms; a second pinch during a `pan` promotes it to a two-hand gesture; with a piece in hand the `pan` does not start.
- **Recenter**: `ui:menu-recenter` → offset `[0, 0]`, scale 0.05, `yawDeg` unchanged; it does not enter the undo history.
- **Declared limit**: with a single hand the menu cannot be used (one-hand Recenter belongs to M5): steps 1–4 and 6–8 use **only `hand-right`** (the left one at rest), step 5 (Recenter) uses both hands.
