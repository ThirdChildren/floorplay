Warnings that emerged in S-MR.3 (they do not change the result):
- **A-MR3-1 · a piece left inside a real volume cannot be grabbed again with the ray.** The piece `armchair#1` released at (1.06; −0.48), almost entirely inside the volume of the couch, was not grabbed again in 4 attempts from different directions (from the upper right, from the front, from the left at z = −0.15): the ray always gave `mr hover … label=couch` and the pinch `mr object selected label=couch` (with the couch card). The logic (`handlePinch` in `src/systems/room-pieces.ts`, lines ~548–549) takes the piece only if the hit distance on the piece does not exceed the one on the real volume; with the piece buried in the couch the volume always wins, and the shelf at (1.10; 0.40) in front prevents reaching it from other directions, so the test is also conditioned by the geometry. A chair left on free floor is grabbed again at the first attempt (`source=room`). Hypothesis: give precedence to the virtual piece when the two hits are within a few cm, or exclude the real volumes from the ray while an invalid piece touches them. To be verified on the headset.
- **A-MR3-2 · 30° cone of the card with the head tilted a lot.** With the head at pitch −57° (looking at the floor at 0.9 m, as when aiming at a near piece), the couch card opened with `coneDeg=49.4`; with pitch ≈ 0 it is 24.4–25.1 and with pitch ≈ −30° it is 25.1; the piece card 19.3. The scenario criterion (≤ 30) holds for the normal gaze; the anchor is computed once at opening (0.55 m in front, upright relative to the head) and does not follow a head tilted by more than ~45°. To be judged on the headset while seated.
- **A-MR3-3 · numbering.** `instance` is unique across all the pieces of the room (`armchair#1`, then `chair#2`, `chair#3`, … `chair#14`), not per `catalogId`; consistent with `room:piece-<catalogId>#<n>` of the scenario, but the number does not restart per type.

## Procedure traps

- No `.mcp.json`: used the `npx @iwsdk/cli` CLI (same set of operations); `browser interact press` for F8/F9.
- **Pinch point `pinch=grip`**: at rest = pose + (−0.029; +0.0015; +0.045); to hit T with a still hand `pose = T + (0.029; −0.0015; −0.045)` works for buttons and menu items (≥ 4 cm); with the pinch already in progress the settled value is T + (0.038; 0.003; −0.037) (M4 memory).
- **MR card (`ui:scan-card`)**: the `Transform` of the entity is the **center** of the panel (not the anchor at the bottom); `Add to Mine`/`Remove` = center + (−0.0505; −0.033; 0), `Close` = center + (+0.088; −0.033; 0), read from `ui inspect` (`relativeCenter` in cm) and from `CARD_SLOTS` in `src/logic/room-view.ts`. A first attempt 9 cm too high did nothing (no error log).
- **Ray aiming**: `xr set-transform` (position) followed by `xr look-at` toward the target; the position without orientation keeps the previous orientation; a `look-at` of the head toward the floor (pitch −57°) changes the cones (A-MR3-2): bring the head back with `look-at` (0; 1.0; −1.5) before opening cards.
- **Hand inside a volume**: with the hand at (1.0; 0.9; 0.6) the pinch hit the shelf (`hover label=storage distance=0.01`, label `Storage`): the virtual hands at rest must not be inside the volumes of the room.
- **Label `Room is full`** lasts 2.5 s and `No room scan found …` 10 s: the screenshot costs ~5 s; read the text with `ui inspect` on the entity `ui:room-notice`, and for the screenshot repeat the gesture and shoot immediately.
- **Menu**: `grabitem` = right at `item + (0.029; −0.0015; −0.045)` identity, `set-select-value 1`, wait 1 s; the menu stays open with the left at `Q_UP` even during the carry (menu dimmed); close it with the left at identity.
- **Cap of 12**: ~12 s per piece with the CLI (8 calls); the same load with MCP would be faster.
- `browser logs` with `level:"error"` also shows errors from earlier loads (shared buffer): compare the timestamps with that of `params source=dev-file`.
