## M5 PC gate verdict

**PC gate passed, with one reservation (F-D-1).**

- All scenarios of the milestone are PASS (A, B, C1, C2, D), `npm test` (6434), `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build` are green, no `CLAUDE.md` violation seen (no Italian string in the interface, no network request by the app, no asset without a license), budget respected with a wide margin (maximum 70 against 100; R-A stays closed).
- No blocker open. The reservation: an intermittent `warn Missing glyph` (F-D-1) that appeared once and was not reproduced in 9 tries. The M5 log contract forbids it to the letter: if the coordinator treats it as a violation, the gate stays open until a reproduction or an explanation; if the coordinator treats it as a warning (it is a `warn`, not an `error`, and the user's rule says the rest is a warning), the gate is passed.
