## Conventions
- Expected colors with **high contrast**: background `#000000`, text `#ffffff`, border `#ffd400`; without: background `#ffffff`, text `#1a1a1a`. They are read with `ui_inspect`.
- Text: normal ≥ 2.4 cm; **large** ≥ 3.0 cm (titles 3.25 cm).
- Hands: `pinch=auto` with the calibration `T + (0; 0.0245; 0.012)` for the whole scenario (the panel rows are small); the S4.1 recipes (long pinch on the washing machine) with `pinch=auto`: use the pose from the log `pinch-start point=` to bring the point onto the target.

## Preconditions
- `dev-params.local.txt` = `house=apartment-a&role=tenant&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1&pinch=auto&time=2026-12-21T10:00` (starting state, without `contrast` or `text`).

## Steps
1. **Normal reference**: standard setup; pinch on `ui:menu-button-right`; `ui_inspect` of the menu (colors, `fontSize`); log `pattern: "menu view|menu opened"` (→ `menu view maxAngleDeg`, `distance`); `browser_screenshot` → attach; close (`ui:menu-button-right`). Open the View panel (`ui:view-button-right`), `ui_inspect`.
2. **High contrast from the panel**: pinch on the `+` of the `Contrast` row (or on the cell `ui:view-contrast`, which toggles; `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:view-contrast` for the ids); log `pattern: "access"` (→ `access contrast=on source=ui`, `access applied recolored=<N>`); `ui_inspect` of the View panel; close, then open the `pinned` menu and the Issues panel: `ui_inspect` of both; `ui_inspect` of the buttons `ui:menu-button-right`, `ui:issue-button-right`, `ui:view-button-right`; `browser_screenshot` of the model → attach (white walls, dark floors, light thresholds, larger pins).
3. **Large text from the panel**: pinch on the `+` of the `Text` row (or on the cell `ui:view-text`); log `pattern: "access|view panel"` (→ `access text=large source=ui … menu=large issues=large`, `view panel view maxAngleDeg=… distance=…`); `ui_inspect` of the View panel (rebuilt at large text). Then, one at a time (the View panel closes by itself):
   - **`pinned` menu** (`ui:menu-button-right`): log `pattern: "menu view|menu opened"`; `ui_inspect` (`fontSize` ≥ 3.0 cm, title 3.25); `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:menu-item-` (→ 6 entries, page 1); pinch on `ui:menu-page-next` twice: three pages (`menu page 1/3 … 3/3`, 14 pieces); close. **Pitch**: repeat the opening with the head tilted by −8°, 0°, +8° (recipe from `qa/scenarios/M4-pinned-menu-pitch.md`) and read `menu view maxAngleDeg`.
   - **Issues panel** (`ui:issue-button-right`): `ui_inspect` of the `home` (`fontSize` ≥ 3.0 cm); `ui:issue-open-list`: list and detail (`issues screen=…`); same entries per page as before (4 rows); close.
   - **Palm menu**: `hand-left` at `L_MENU` with `Q_UP` (wait 0.9 s): log `menu opened hand=left mode=palm` and `menu view`; `ui_inspect`; close.
   - **Labels**: select a room with a short pinch (room label), take a piece so that the FitCheck label appears (`my-sofa` toward `living`, S3.3): `ui_inspect` of the labels; `ecs_query_entity` for the distances from the head.
   - `browser_screenshot` of each → attach.
4. **Persistence and parameters**: wait 1.5 s; rewrite the file **without** `reset=1` and **without** `contrast`/`text`, reload: log `pattern: "access|state restored"`; then rewrite with `contrast=0&text=normal` (explicit): log `pattern: "access"`.
5. **The whole interface**: with contrast and large text active (rewrite the file with `contrast=1&text=large`): role `tenant`, long pinch on the washing machine (S4.1 step 2), `ui_inspect` of the new-issue panel; `ui:issue-cancel`; real scale and back (`ui:tabletop-button`, `ui_inspect`).
6. **One hand only**: all the panels and buttons above (except the palm menu of step 3, which requires the left hand by construction) open with the right hand alone; the left at rest.
7. Unfiltered log (`error`, `uncaught`, `Cannot read`, `reading 'add'`, `Missing glyph`); cleanup.

## Checks (all mandatory)
1. **Step 1**: reference: white panels with text `#1a1a1a`; `fontSize` ≥ 2.4 cm; `menu view maxAngleDeg` ≈ 26.1° at 0.59 m.
2. **Step 2**: `access contrast=on source=ui`, `access applied recolored=<n>` with n ≥ 1; all the panels and buttons opened afterwards have background `#000000`, text `#ffffff`, border `#ffd400`; the **fixed buttons already present** are recolored **in place** (`setProperties`, not recreated) without errors; the model: walls `#ffffff`, floors darker than before, door thresholds lighter (comparison of the screenshots and, if available, `Material` read via `ecs_query_entity`); the pins and the pinch point ≥ 30 % larger. The test `theme-contrast.test.ts` guarantees ≥ 7:1 for every pair: the gate cites it (`npm test`).
3. **Step 3 (large text)**: the line `access text=large source=ui … menu=large issues=large`; in **all** the elements listed below `fontSize` ≥ **3.0 cm** (titles 3.25): View panel (40 × 41 cm, 3.8 cm rows, `view panel view maxAngleDeg` ≈ **27.8°** at 0.59 m), `pinned` menu and Issues panel (45 × 47 cm, 6 entries per page and 3 pages for the 14 pieces, **not** a 2 × 2 grid), fixed buttons (width ×1.25, pinch rectangles included), Tabletop button, labels (room, FitCheck, reason, tape measure) and hints. **Cone**: `menu view maxAngleDeg` ≈ **28.5°** looking straight ahead and **≤ 30.0°** with pitch −8°, 0°, +8°; center distance ≈ 0.60 m (frame at 0.64 m), within the 0.50–0.80 m range; the Issues panel gives the same (`issues panel view maxAngleDeg`). **Palm menu**: distance 0.58–0.68 m and maximum angle **30.0°** at 0.66 m (tolerance 0.1° for rounding; more than 30.1° is a warning to report). **Labels**: minimum distance from the head **≥ 0.58 m** with large text (room label ≈ 57 cm, FitCheck ≈ 55 cm wide, inside the cone). **Known limit**: the pitch window that stays in the cone narrows to ±8° (at normal text −14°/+12°): beyond ±8° the edge may exceed 30°, it is a warning, not FAIL.
3b. **Panel log lines**: every press on `Contrast`/`Text` writes `view step row=contrast value=on|off` and `view step row=text value=large|normal`, then `access …`; the line `access text=… source=… menu=large issues=large` always reports `menu=large issues=large` (even with `text=normal`: the two fields say that the panels *know how* to follow the preference, not that they are large at that moment).
4. **Step 4**: on reload without parameters `access contrast=on source=saved` and `access text=large source=saved`; with explicit `contrast=0&text=normal` `access contrast=off source=url` and `access text=normal source=url` and everything goes back to normal (the explicit value wins over the saved one **even with `0`/`normal`**); an older save without the `contrast`/`largeText`/`coach` fields is read with the default values.
5. **Step 5**: the new-issue panel and the Tabletop button respect high contrast and large text; no text cut off outside the panel (`ui_inspect`: no child beyond the edge).
6. **Step 6**: no `pinch left` line and no `menu opened hand=left` except the one of the palm menu in step 3.
7. **Menu v2 at large text (delivered)**: same model as the normal one scaled ×1.25 — 6 entries per page, 3 pages, four tabs, `Undo`/`Back`/`Next`/`Recenter` bar — with `fontSize` ≥ 3.0 cm, cone ≤ 30° (check 3) and no entry cut off (`ui_inspect`: no child beyond the edge). Note: the log line always reports `menu=large issues=large` (see 3b); the proof that the menu is really large is the `fontSize` ≥ 3.0 cm read with `ui_inspect` and the measured cone: if `fontSize` is < 3.0 cm after `text=large`, it is a **FAIL** (no longer a warning).
8. No `error` entry; no `Missing glyph`; no `reading 'add'`; no network request.
9. **Headset only** (high): real legibility in the narrow field of view; the pure white of the walls does not dazzle; the 3.0 cm text is enough; the 45 × 47 cm panel does not cover too much of the model.

PASS if checks 1–8 are met · FAIL otherwise.
