**Emulator recipes (verified by the engineer on 2026-11-07, headless)**: cycle `write dev-params.local.txt` → `browser reload` → wait 9 s → `xr enter` → `xr set-input-mode {"mode":"hand"}` → wait 10 s; on cloud IWER 6–8 fps (SwiftShader, `hz=90 hzFrom=session`): the fps have **no** meaning, what counts are format, calls and positions. CLI names: `xr enter`, `xr exit` (not `end-session`). `browser logs` gives at most 200 lines, the oldest first and mixed between reloads: cut after the last `Frame navigated` (jq + awk) and filter with `pattern`. Editing a `.uikitml` or a source reloads the page and loses the XR session. The **F7** key (with `debug=1`) opens and closes the `pinned` menu; without `debug=1` a pinch on `ui:menu-button-right` is used (hand = button position − `D_GRIP`, `xr select` 0.3 s). Marker recipe: world position = `miniature:root.pos + scale × (local + house position)` with the house shifted by (−5.5; 0; −3.6): V2 at (0.065; 1.4375; −0.47) at the default placement.

**Gate log contract** (in addition to that of M5): no `error` except **`kind: requestfailed` `GET …/tuning.local.txt net::ERR_ABORTED`** (artifact of a reload during a poll); accepted `warn`s: those deliberately provoked by S6.1 (clamp, unknown name, order, `needs-debug`, `not-a-query`) and S6.2 (`param time`, `param hud ignored`), plus `THREE.WebGLRenderer: Can't change size while VR device is presenting.` after `xr exit`. No `Missing glyph`, `reading 'add'`, `budget exceeded`, `uncaught`.

**Criterion differences already decided** (not FAILs): `ua=` in the emulator is a Quest user agent; the IWER probe line has the prefix `[soglia] env iwer clues`, not `[soglia:env]`; the source of the tuning values in the emulator is `file` (not `url`); the live file without `t_` is ignored without a log; `mode=immersive-vr` in the `session` line is derived from `environmentBlendMode`; the HUD costs +2 calls (limit +3); the K2 base with 14 pieces is 50 instead of 46 (and K3 58, K12 26, K18 68: the numbers of `M5-budget.md` cannot be reproduced even on M5, see the rule of step 4).
