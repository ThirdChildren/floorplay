# S2.6 · Door blocked by a piece of furniture

> **Changelog 2026-11-02** (starting point only, criteria unchanged; M3 gate, `qa/reports/M3-2026-10-11-C1-m2-regressions.md`): step 6: the menu **closes first** before `furniture:armchair#1` is picked up again from the room center, because with menu v2 open the pinch at `W(6.8; 2.5)` (0.075 m from the button) is taken by `ui:menu-recenter`; "menu held up for the whole scenario" applies until step 5.

**Milestone**: M2 · **Type**: PC (IWER emulator via MCP `iwsdk-runtime`)
Implementation prerequisites: T2.4, T2.5, T2.9, T2.13 (see `docs/plans/M2.md`, D16: 0.30 m free zone in front of and behind every door).

## Preconditions
- As S2.2: `dev-params.local.txt` = `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1`, reload, accept, hands, head (0; 1.6; 0), O from the last `miniature placed` line, menu open (`hand-left` at `L_MENU` with `Q_UP`, held up). `W(px, pz)` as in S2.2.
- The door `door:d-bedroom` is in wall `w-bedroom-hall` (z = 4.6, thickness 0.12), opening from x = 6.0 to 6.8; its free zone is x 6.0–6.8, z from 4.24 to 4.96.
- Points (O = (0; 1.35; −0.45), y = 1.39): **PD** = `W(6.4; 4.0)` = (0.045; 1.39; −0.430) → armchair 0.8 × 0.8 snapped to (6.40; 4.14), exactly in front of the door; **PS** = `W(7.5; 4.0)` = (0.100; 1.39; −0.430) → snapped to (7.50; 4.14), next to the door (x 7.1–7.9); **PC** = `W(6.8; 2.5)` = (0.065; 1.39; −0.505) → center of the room.

## Steps
1. Menu open. **Armchair on the door**: `ui:menu-item-armchair` (page 1) → `I`; grab at `I` (orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)`, select 1), `xr_animate_to` to **PD**, wait 1 s. **In flight**: `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:armchair#1` with `["Furniture"]`; log `pattern: "furniture status"`. `browser_screenshot` (red outline in front of the door, the door threshold in orange below). `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^ui:reason-"` and `ecs_query_entity` on the label found with `["Transform"]` (for distance and cone, check 8); log `pattern: "reason"`.
2. Release. Log `pattern: "furniture placed|furniture status|reason"`; `ecs_query_entity` with `["Furniture"]`; state; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:reason-`.
3. **Armchair next to the door**: second armchair (`ui:menu-item-armchair`), grab and release at **PS**. Log and `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:armchair#2`; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:reason-` (only the label of the first armchair).
4. **Move the first armchair away from the door**: grab `furniture:armchair#1` from its center `W(6.4; 4.14)` = (0.045; 1.39; **−0.423**) (pz = Oz + (4.14 − 3.6)·0.05 = −0.45 + 0.027 = −0.423; the previous version wrote −0.428, i.e. a grab 0.10 m of plan above the center: arithmetic error), `xr_animate_to` to **PC**, release. Log and `Furniture`.
5. `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:` and with `^ui:reason-`.
6. **An older piece moved onto a newer one (new behavior of `evaluateAll`: the moved piece becomes invalid, not the one standing still)**. `furniture:armchair#1` (placed first, now at about (6.80; 2.50)) is **older** than `furniture:armchair#2` (at (7.50; 4.14), standing still). **Close the menu** (`hand-left` palm down, wait 1 s: with the menu open the `ui:menu-recenter` button wins over the piece). Grab `furniture:armchair#1` from its center `W(x; z)` with `x`, `z` from the `furniture placed furniture:armchair#1 …` line of step 4 (expected about `W(6.8; 2.5)` = (0.065; 1.39; −0.505)); `xr_animate_to` to `W(7.5; 4.14)` = (0.100; 1.39; −0.423) (the center of the second armchair; hand displacement = target − grab center, as in step 4), wait 1 s. **In flight**: `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:armchair#1` and on `furniture:armchair#2` with `["Furniture"]`; `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "furniture status|reason"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:reason-`. Release, wait 0.5 s; log `pattern: "furniture placed|furniture status|reason"`; `Furniture` of both; state; `browser_screenshot` (red outline on the moved armchair, no outline on the other).
7. `browser_get_console_logs` without a filter (look for `error`).

3. Step 3: `furniture placed furniture:armchair#2 room=bedroom x=7.50 z=4.14 rot=0 status=valid` (x = 7.50 ± 0.10, z = 4.14 ± 0.10); `outline` = `none` (next to the opening, outside the zone).
4. Step 4: `furniture status furniture:armchair#1 status=valid reasons=-`; `furniture placed furniture:armchair#1 room=bedroom x=6.80 z=2.50 rot=0 status=valid` (x = 6.80 ± 0.10, z = 2.50 ± 0.10: in the M2 gate z = 2.60 came out, due to the wrong grab of step 4 off by 0.10 m of plan, see Changelog); `outline` = `none`.
5. Step 5: exactly **2** `furniture:*` entities; `[soglia:state]`: `nextInstance` `{"armchair":3}`.
6. No `room selected` line, no `miniature gesture start`; no `error` entry (read at step 7).
7. Screenshot of step 1 attached and described in words.
8. **Readable reason (D27, T2.9b)**: step 1 (in flight): **1** `ui:reason-armchair#1` entity and the line `[soglia] reason shown furniture:armchair#1 "Blocks the door"` (exact text); step 2: the label stays after the release; step 3: the second armchair (valid) has **no** label (`ui:reason-armchair#2` absent); step 4: after the move `reason hidden furniture:armchair#1` and **0** `ui:reason-*` entities at step 5; the label is at **0.52 m or more** (`LABEL_DISTANCE_MARGIN`; rounding tolerance 0.001) and at **≤ 0.65 m** from the head (in rerun 2 it was at 0.5000 m, i.e. at the old limit), **inside the central 30° cone** (`acos(−dz / |d|)` ≤ 30° with `d` = label position − (0; 1.6; 0), from the `Transform` read at step 1 and after the release), **yaw-only**, and in the screenshot of step 1 the text "Blocks the door" is **fully readable** (not cut off by the edge of the view nor covered by the dimmed menu). For the `Overlaps the armchair` label of step 6 the same checks apply (distance ≥ 0.52 m, cone ≤ 30°, whole text). Numeric deviations (0.515 m, 30.5°) are warnings; cut-off or unreadable text is FAIL (rule 8).

9. **The moved piece is the one that becomes invalid (step 6)**: in flight and after the release the line `[soglia] furniture status furniture:armchair#1 status=invalid reasons=overlaps-furniture with=furniture:armchair#2` appears (**with this id and with `with=furniture:armchair#2`**, exact text; the reasons contain neither `blocks-door` nor `overlaps-wall`); `furniture:armchair#1` has `Furniture.status` = `invalid`, `outline` = `red`, and after the release `furniture placed furniture:armchair#1 room=bedroom x=7.50 z=4.14 rot=0 status=invalid` (x and z within ± 0.10: the snap to the south wall brings the center to z = 4.14); **`furniture:armchair#2` (standing still, placed later)** stays `valid`, `outline` = `none`, and **no `furniture status furniture:armchair#2 … invalid` line appears**; there is **1** single `ui:reason-armchair#1` entity with the line `[soglia] reason shown furniture:armchair#1 "Overlaps the armchair"` (exact text) and **no** `ui:reason-armchair#2`; at the end **2** `furniture:*` entities and `nextInstance` still `{"armchair":3}` (the move does not create instances). The rule is also in the unit tests of `evaluateAll` (`tests/unit/furniture-diff.test.ts`, group "who is marked when pieces collide after a move"), which run with `npm test`. If instead armchair #2 (the piece standing still) turns out `invalid`: FAIL.

## Outcome
PASS if checks 1–6, 8 and 9 are met · FAIL otherwise. If the armchair is marked `invalid` for `overlaps-wall` instead of `blocks-door`, FAIL (the flush snap must give interpenetration ≤ 0.005 m).
