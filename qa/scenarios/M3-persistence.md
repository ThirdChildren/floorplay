## Common conventions (also valid for the other M3 scenarios)
- Setup as S2.1: write `dev-params.local.txt`, `browser_reload_page`, `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode {mode:"hand"}`, wait 2 s; head at (0; 1.6; 0); `O` = (Ox; Oy; Oz) from the last `miniature placed` line (expected (0; 1.35; −0.45)); scale 0.05.
- **`pinch=grip` in all M3 scenarios except S3.6 and the "auto" regression gate**: the pinch point and the palm stay those of M2, so the known constants apply: `D_GRIP` = (−0.038; −0.003; +0.037), hand pose to hit the point `T` = `T − D_GRIP` = (Tx + 0.038; Ty + 0.003; Tz − 0.037) with orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)`; `Q_UP` = `(0, −0.2588, 0.9659, 0)`; `L_MENU` = (−0.25; 1.15; −0.20), `R_MENU` = (0.25; 1.15; −0.20). If T3.2b changed the constants, the report cites those of `docs/plans/M3.md` ("Spike outcomes") and the scenario uses `pinch=auto` with them.
- Floor plan → world conversion (house A, scale 0.05, yaw 0, offset 0): `W(px, pz, h)` = (Ox + (px − 5.5)·0.05; Oy + h; Oz + (pz − 3.6)·0.05), `h` = 0.04 for objects on the ground. House B: center (4.0; 3.45) instead of (5.5; 3.6) (to be confirmed by reading the local `Transform` of `house:apartment-b`, which equals −center).
- Known M2 poses (A): P1 (bed in the bedroom) hand at (0.103; 1.393; −0.602) → pose at 6.80 / 1.125; bed center to re-grab (0.103; 1.393; −0.611).
- All waits are read from the `ts` of the logs; every MCP call costs 0.5-3 s. To read only the current load use `since` or the `ts` of the line `params source=`.
- **Gate rule (user, 2026-10-06)**: only console errors in the normal path, red tests/build, violations of `CLAUDE.md` and > 100 draw calls per view block; numeric or aesthetic deviations are warnings. The **functional** criteria below (a lost piece, a duplicate, a grab left open, an error) remain FAIL.
- **Tool limit** (verified in `node_modules/@iwsdk/cli/dist/contract.js`, 2026-10-09): no MCP tool reads `localStorage` or changes `visibilityState`. The proof of persistence is the **page reload** plus the lines `state saved` / `state restored`; suspension is tested with `browser_interact` `press` `F8` (hidden) and `F9` (visible), which call **the same function** `applyVisibility` as the real event (D30): the report declares that the headset event remains **headset only**.

## Preconditions
- Phase 1-3: `dev-params.local.txt` = `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1&pinch=grip`.
- Menu opened with `hand-left` at `L_MENU` with `Q_UP` (wait 0.9 s); the right takes from the menu and places as in S2.2.

## Steps
1. **Phase 1 · save**. Load with `reset=1`: `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "state|params"`. Place **two pieces**: `bed-double` (menu page 1, item `ui:menu-item-bed-double`) at P1 and `armchair` (item `ui:menu-item-armchair`) in the living room on a free point `W(1.5; 3.0)`; rotate the bed by 90° with the left-hand tap before the release (S2.5). Note from the `furniture placed` lines the final poses (x, z, rot) → `S1_bed`, `S1_arm`. Wait **1.2 s** after the last release. Log `pattern: "state saved|furniture placed|state"`, `count: 10`; most recent `[soglia:state]` line → `historyLength`, `nextInstance`, `prefs.onboardingStep`.
2. **Phase 2 · reload without `reset`**. Rewrite `dev-params.local.txt` = `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&seed=1&debug=1&pinch=grip` (**without `reset=1`**), `browser_reload_page`, `xr_accept_session`, hands, wait 2.5 s. `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`; `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:bed-double#1` and `furniture:armchair#1` with `["Furniture", "Transform"]`; log `pattern: "state|furnish|miniature placed|onboarding"`; `[soglia:state]` line.
   - Then pinch on `ui:menu-undo` (menu open as in step 1, right on the button with the grip correction): it must undo the last saved action.
   - Then place a **second** `bed-double` from the menu in the bedroom on a free point `W(7.0; 3.0)` (hand at (0.113; 1.393; −0.517); correction 2026-11-02). **Before continuing** read `furniture placed … status=valid` (correction 2026-11-06); if it is not `valid`, choose another free point of the bedroom (and note it in the report).
3. **Phase 3 · save on suspension**. Move a piece (grab from the miniature and release 0.5 m of plan further) and **immediately** `browser_interact` `press` `F8` (hidden). Log `pattern: "lifecycle|state saved"`. Then `press` `F9`. Log.
4. **Phase 4 · `reset=1` deletes**. Rewrite the file **with** `reset=1`, reload, accept, hands, wait 2.5 s. `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`; log `pattern: "state"`. Then rewrite **without** `reset=1`, reload, accept, hands, wait 2.5 s: `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`; log `pattern: "state"`.
5. **Phase 5 · one house does not inherit from the other**. With the file without `reset`: load A, place a `bed-double` (wait 1.2 s: `state saved key=…apartment-a`), then rewrite with `house=apartment-b` (still without `reset`), reload, accept, hands, wait 2.5 s: `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`; log `pattern: "state"`. Then go back to `house=apartment-a` (without `reset`), reload, accept, hands: `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`; log.
6. **Phase 6 · hidden session with a piece from the MENU in hand**. Load A with `reset=1` (file with `reset=1`, reload, accept, hands, wait 2 s), place a `bed-double` in the bedroom (it stays in the store); open the menu, take `ui:menu-item-armchair` with the right (grip correction, `xr_set_select_value` `hand-right` = 1, wait 0.4 s: `furniture grabbed furniture:armchair#1 source=menu hand=right`), `xr_animate_to` above the living room `W(1.5; 3.0)` in 0.8 s, wait 0.6 s, **without releasing**. T6a readings: `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:` (bed + armchair in hand) and with `^ui:furniture-preview`; `[soglia:state]` line. Then `press` `F8`. Wait 1 s. T6b readings: `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:` and `^ui:furniture-preview`; log `pattern: "lifecycle|grab|pinch|state saved|menu"` (with `since`); `[soglia:state]`. Then `xr_set_select_value` `hand-right` = 0 (the IWER hand is still "closed": the end arrives after the suspension), wait 0.6 s; T6c readings: log, `^furniture:`. Then `press` `F9`, wait 0.5 s: log `pattern: "lifecycle"`. **Resume**: `hand-left` palm up (`L_MENU` with `Q_UP`), wait **1.2 s** (300 ms guard + 0.4 s); `menu opened`; new grab of `ui:menu-item-armchair` with the right, carry to `W(1.5; 3.0)`, release. T6d readings: log `pattern: "furniture|lifecycle"`, `^furniture:`, `Furniture` of the armchair.
7. **Phase 7 · hidden session with a piece from the MINIATURE in hand**. From the state of phase 6: re-grab the bed from its center (S2.2, step 7), `xr_animate_to` 0.5 m of plan further right, **without releasing**; T7a readings: `Furniture` of the bed (`phase: held`), `x`/`z`. `press` `F8`, wait 1 s: T7b readings: `^furniture:`, `Furniture` and `Transform` of the bed (new entity index: repeat `ecs_find_entities` first), `[soglia:state]` (`historyLength`), log. `xr_set_select_value` 0, wait 0.6 s; `press` `F9`; T7c readings. Then re-grab and place the bed elsewhere (a new action: +1 in `historyLength`).
8. **Phase 8 · end of session with a piece in hand**. Take from the menu a `chair` (`ui:menu-item-chair`, page 1; the store already contains the bed and `furniture:armchair#1` from phase 6) and keep it in hand; `xr_end_session`; wait 1 s; log `pattern: "grab|pinch|lifecycle|state saved|session"`; `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode {mode:"hand"}`, wait 2 s; `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`; `^ui:furniture-preview`; `[soglia:state]` line.
9. `browser_get_console_logs` unfiltered, looking for `error`, `uncaught`, `Cannot read`, `QuotaExceeded`. `browser_screenshot` at step 2 (house restored) → attach.
10. Cleanup: select 0 on both, hands far away, `xr_end_session`, delete `dev-params.local.txt`.

## Checks (all mandatory)
1. **Phase 1**: the line `[soglia] state none key=soglia:v1:state:apartment-a` or `state cleared reason=reset keys=<n>` (n ≥ 0) exists before the first piece; after the last release the line `[soglia] state saved key=soglia:v1:state:apartment-a pieces=2 bytes=<n> reason=debounce` appears with `bytes` > 0, and its `ts` is between **0.45 s and 1.5 s** after the last `furniture placed` line (500 ms debounce; ±0.05 s is a warning). A `state saved` line does not appear for every micro-change (at most 1 per release). The test of merging 5 changes in 400 ms is a **unit** test (MCP latency too high): the report declares it.
2. **Phase 2 (restore)**: line `[soglia] state restored pieces=2 key=soglia:v1:state:apartment-a` (before `miniature placed` or in any case before the creation of the pieces); exactly **2** `furniture:*` entities: `furniture:bed-double#1` and `furniture:armchair#1`, with `x`, `z`, `rotationDeg` equal to `S1_bed` and `S1_arm` (± 0.005 m; identical rotation); `status` `valid`; `[soglia:state]` has `historyLength` equal to that of phase 1, `nextInstance` with `bed-double` 2 and `armchair` 2, and `prefs.onboardingStep` and `menuOpened` as in phase 1 (with `menuOpened:true` no `hint shown` line). **Not** restored: `miniature.scale` 0.05, `offset` [0,0], `yawDeg` 0 and `miniature placed` at the anchor O; `selectedRoomId` null; no `room selected` line. Undo undoes the last saved action (`undo action=place id=furniture:armchair#1`; the piece disappears, the bed stays). The new `bed-double` has id **`furniture:bed-double#2`** (never `#1` reused), `furniture placed … status=valid`.
3. **Phase 3 (suspension)**: after `F8` the line `[soglia] lifecycle suspended source=debug-key state=hidden`; the moved piece is in the saved state: either `state saved … reason=hidden` appears or the last `state saved` (debounce) already has `pieces` equal to the number of pieces in the store. After `F9` the line `lifecycle resumed source=debug-key state=visible`. No `error`.
4. **Phase 4 (`reset=1`)**: with `reset=1` the line `[soglia] state cleared reason=reset keys=<n>` with n ≥ 1 (the keys created in the previous phases) and **0** `furniture:*` entities; at the next reload **without** `reset=1` **0** `furniture:*` entities and one of the two lines: `state none key=soglia:v1:state:apartment-a` **or** `state restored pieces=0 key=soglia:v1:state:apartment-a` (since the M4 issue seed the load with `reset=1` saves the state, so the second is the normal one; correction 2026-11-06). The `reset` really deleted the pieces: no `state restored` line with `pieces` ≥ 1.
5. **Phase 5 (one house per key)**: in `apartment-b` the line `state none key=soglia:v1:state:apartment-b` (or `cleared`) and **0** `furniture:*` entities; on returning to `apartment-a` the line `state restored pieces=1 key=…apartment-a` and **1** entity `furniture:bed-double#1`.
6. **Phase 6 (menu piece, D30)**: T6a: 2 `furniture:*` entities (`bed-double#1`, `armchair#1` with `phase` `held`), 1 `ui:furniture-preview`. T6b: **1** `furniture:*` entity (only the bed; **0** `furniture:armchair#*`), **0** `ui:furniture-preview` entities, store with 1 piece and `nextInstance.armchair` **not consumed** (absent or 1) and `historyLength` unchanged; lines in `ts` order (non-decreasing): `lifecycle suspended source=debug-key state=hidden`, (if the state had changed) `state saved … reason=hidden`, `furniture grab cancelled furniture:armchair#1 reason=suspend`, `pinch right end`; **no** `furniture placed`/`furniture returned` line; the menu is closed (0 `ui:palm-menu`, line `menu closed`). T6c (select 0 after the suspension): **no** new `furniture placed`/`furniture returned` line, no error, 1 `furniture:*` entity. Resume: `lifecycle resumed source=debug-key state=visible`; the menu does **not** open by itself (0 `ui:palm-menu` right after `F9`), it opens with the palm up after 0.7 s or more (≥ 0.68 s) from the line `lifecycle resumed`; the new grab produces `furniture grabbed furniture:armchair#1 source=menu hand=right` with **the same id** as before and, on release, `furniture placed furniture:armchair#1 …`: 2 `furniture:*` entities, no id skipped.
7. **Phase 7 (miniature piece, D30)**: T7a: the bed has `phase: held`. T7b: **1** entity `furniture:bed-double#1` with `phase: placed` and **pose equal to the one it had at the grab** (x, z, rot ± 0.005; **not** the hand pose), `historyLength` **unchanged** from before the grab (no `moveFurniture`), line `furniture grab cancelled furniture:bed-double#1 reason=suspend`, **0** `ui:furniture-preview`, no `furniture placed`/`furniture returned`/`undo` line. T7c: no change for the late release. After the resume a new grab and placement elsewhere works (+1 in `historyLength`).
8. **Phase 8 (end of session)**: after `xr_end_session`, line `furniture grab cancelled furniture:chair#1 reason=suspend` or `reason=session-end` (one of the two; the report says which), no `error`; in the new session `^furniture:` contains only the pieces **already placed** in the store (the bed and `furniture:armchair#1`), **0** `ui:furniture-preview`, **0** `furniture:chair#*` entities (no duplicate, no orphan piece) and a grab from the menu works (`furniture grabbed furniture:chair#1`).
9. No `error` entries in any phase; no `QuotaExceeded` line; no `feature localStorage unavailable` line (in the emulator `localStorage` exists).
10. The report declares plainly: (a) `localStorage` absent/blocked/quota full and the debounce merging are proven **only by unit tests** (`tests/unit/persistence.test.ts`, `storage.test.ts`); (b) `F8`/`F9` **simulate** the visibility event, they do not prove that the Quest emits it; (c) the headset tests are in `qa/device/DEBT.md`.

## Outcome
PASS if checks 1-9 are met and 10 is in the report · FAIL otherwise. A `ts` outside the debounce window by ±0.05 s is a warning.
