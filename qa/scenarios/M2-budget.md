# S2.8 · Graphics budget with 14 furniture pieces (test preset)

**Milestone**: M2 · **Type**: PC (IWER emulator via MCP `iwsdk-runtime`); fps are **informational only**
Implementation prerequisites: T2.5 (valid preset **after the data correction approved by the user**, D26), T2.9, T2.9b and T2.14/T2.15 for the worst case, T2.18 (see `docs/plans/M2.md`, D16, D20, D23, D26, D27). Extends `qa/scenarios/M1-budget.md`: the same reading rules apply (`callsPerView = calls / views`, `triangles` = total of the two views).

## Preconditions
- Consecutive runs, changing `dev-params.local.txt` and doing `browser_reload_page` between one and the next:
  - **Run 1**: `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1&furnish=scandinavian`
  - **Run 2**: `house=apartment-b&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1&furnish=scandinavian`
  - **Run 3** (reference without furniture): `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1`
  - **Run 4** (block fallback, models that fail): `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1&failmodels=1&furnish=scandinavian` (`failmodels=1` works only with `debug=1`; without it, the line `param failmodels ignored: needs debug=1` and the models load).
- For each: `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode` with `mode: "hand"`, wait 7 s; head at (0; 1.6; 0).
- The `staging.scandinavian` preset of `apartment-a.json` has 14 pieces, **all valid with the D16 rules** after the T2.5 correction (`bookcase` at x = 10.69 and `sofa-3seat` at z = 3.10, or the minimum values chosen by the test; the report cites the real values found in the file): `sofa-3seat` ×1, `coffee-table` ×1, `table-dining` ×1, `chair` ×4, `bed-double` ×1, `nightstand` ×2, `wardrobe` ×1, `desk` ×1, `bookcase` ×1, `plant` ×1. `apartment-b.json` has no `staging`.

## Steps
1. Run 1: `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "furnish|catalog|soglia:stats"`, `count: 30`; `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^furniture:"`.
2. `scene_get_render_stats` (note `calls`, `triangles`, `geometries`, `textures`, `programs`, `meshCount`, `shadowCasters`).
3. `browser_profile` with `action: "start"`, `mode: "rendering"`, `maxDurationMs: 5000`; wait 5 s; `action: "stop"` (informational).
4. **Worst case, measured at TWO scales: 0.05 (run 1, right after step 3) and 0.12 (after the zoom)**. For each scale three measurements in a row, each with a 3 s wait and `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "soglia:stats"`, `count: 10` (the **last** line is read; `callsPerView` = `calls` / views, rule of `qa/scenarios/M1-budget.md`):
   - **M-closed**: menu closed, no piece in hand (here `scene_get_render_stats` works and is compared with the line, as in S1.4 check 5).
   - **M-open**: menu open (`hand-left` at `L_MENU` with `Q_UP`, wait 0.9 s + 3 s), no piece in hand.
   - **M-worst**: menu open **and a piece in hand in an invalid pose with the reason label visible**: grab `ui:menu-item-armchair` (right hand, grip correction as in S2.1 step 9c), `xr_animate_to` above `furniture:bed-double#1` (at scale 0.05: `W(px, pz)` of the bed, y = Oy + 0.04; at scale 0.12: position of the bed read with `ecs_query_entity` `["Transform"]` + (0; 0.05; 0), to be adjusted by at most ±0.03 m in y twice if the label does not appear), **without releasing**, wait 3 s. **Mandatory check that the label has actually appeared before reading the numbers**: `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:reason-` ≥ 1 (expected `ui:reason-armchair#1`) **and** line `[soglia] reason shown furniture:armchair#1 "…"` (reason text in English; the exact text does not decide) in the log; if missing, the measurement is **not valid**: repeat once and declare in the report that the label was not there (it is not reported as "worst case"). Then release outside the miniature (the piece returns; `furniture returned … from=menu`) and menu down.
   - **Zoom (0.05 → 0.12)**: phase C of `qa/scenarios/M1-two-hands.md`, step 10 (the scale reached is read from the `[soglia:state]`/`miniature` line, expected 0.12); then M-closed, M-open, M-worst are repeated.
   - **With the menu open `scene_get_render_stats` may fail** (`Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'x')`, reproduced in the gate; it works with the menu closed): **do not use it for M-open and M-worst**, the source is the `[soglia:stats]` line (finding F2, a tool problem, not an app problem; no error in console). A failure of the tool is not an app FAIL.
   - Reference values of rerun 2 with run 1 (A + 14 pieces) to find the same conditions again: scale 0.05 → **49 / 58 / 66** (closed / open / open + piece + label); scale 0.12 → **40 / 46 / 48**.
5. Run 2: wait 7 s, `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "furnish|soglia:stats"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`.
6. Run 3: wait 7 s, `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "soglia:stats"`, `count: 10` (reference).
7. For each run: `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "budget exceeded|framework:|Missing glyph|uncaught|unhandled"` and without a filter (look for `error`). Note the line `[soglia] font ready …` if it exists.
8. After run 1: `browser_screenshot` (furnished house, head pointed at the miniature with `xr_look_at` toward O).
9. **Run 4 (block fallback)**: wait 7 s; `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "furniture models|furniture model unavailable|failmodels|furnish|soglia:stats"`, `count: 40`; `ecs_find_entities` with `^furniture:`; `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:sofa-3seat#1` and on `furniture:bookcase#1` with `["Furniture"]`; `scene_get_render_stats` (menu closed: it works; if it failed, the `[soglia:stats]` line); `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "budget exceeded|framework:|Missing glyph|uncaught|unhandled"` and without a filter (look for `error`); `browser_screenshot` (furnished house with **blocks** in place of the models, described in words).

## Checks (all mandatory)
1. Run 1: line `[soglia] furnish applied style=scandinavian pieces=14 invalid=0`; exactly **14** `furniture:*` entities with ids `furniture:sofa-3seat#1`, `coffee-table#1`, `table-dining#1`, `chair#1`–`#4`, `bed-double#1`, `nightstand#1`–`#2`, `wardrobe#1`, `desk#1`, `bookcase#1`, `plant#1`; all with `Furniture.status` = `valid` and `reasons` = `-` (`ecs_query_entity` on each, or the absence of `furniture status … invalid` lines in the log) and **no** `ui:reason-*` entity; the sofa has `z` ≈ 3.10 (≤ 3.19) and the bookcase `x` ≈ 10.69 (≤ 10.70), read from `ecs_query_entity` on `furniture:sofa-3seat#1` and `furniture:bookcase#1` with `["Furniture"]` (with the original data 3.42 and 10.8 the `furnish applied` line would have `invalid=2`).
2. Run 2 (house B): `[soglia] furnish: no staging in apartment-b` (level `warn`), **0** `furniture:*` entities, no `error`, no error panel.
3. **Budget (decides the outcome, BLOCKS)**, on the last `[soglia:stats]` line of every run and of **every measurement of step 4 (M-closed, M-open, M-worst, at scale 0.05 and at 0.12)**: **`callsPerView`** ≤ 100 and `triangles` ≤ 150,000. More than 100 draw calls per view in **any** measurement: FAIL.
4. **M2 internal targets** (**non-blocking WARNINGS**, they do not decide and do **not** cause a FAIL): (a) `callsPerView` ≤ 70 and `triangles` ≤ 60,000 with the 14 pieces, **in every measurement of step 4** (if > 70: write "WARNING: n > 70" with the measurement, never FAIL); (b) **menu cost** = `callsPerView(M-open) − callsPerView(M-closed)` at the same scale: **expected ~ +9** (rerun 2: 49 → 58 at 0.05; 40 → 46 at 0.12); **WARNING if > 12**; (c) the report also gives the difference from run 3 (M1 baseline: `callsPerView` ≈ 35, `triangles` ≈ 21.6k). The old "accepted 74" (user decision of 2026-10-06, `qa/reports/M2-2026-10-06.md`, W1) is **no longer needed as a reference**: the expected worst case is **66** at 0.05 and **48** at 0.12; only the blocking cap of 100 and the warning at 70 remain. The worst case must still be measured on the Quest (`qa/device/DEBT.md`).
5. Textures: **app textures ≤ 4** in total and each ≤ 1024 px (font atlases from the `font ready` log if present; the glTFs of the models must share a single texture); framework textures are listed as in S1.4 check 6b with no limit; **0** `budget exceeded` lines; the `framework: …` line only as in S1.4 check 6e.
6. `shadowCasters` = 0 in every `scene_get_render_stats`; no `error` entry in console; no `Missing glyph info`.
7. Consistency with `scene_get_render_stats`: `calls` equal to that of the `[soglia:stats]` line (as S1.4 check 5), **in the measurements with the menu closed**. With the **menu open** the tool may fail (see step 4): for M-open and M-worst the `[soglia:stats]` line is the only source and the report declares it; the tool failure is not an app FAIL.
8. The report contains a table with `callsPerView`, `triangles`, `textures`, `fps` and frame time for each run and for **the six measurements of step 4** (M-closed / M-open / M-worst × scale 0.05 and 0.12), the computed menu cost (check 4b), the proof that the label was present in M-worst (`ui:reason-*` id and `reason shown` line), and states plainly: "fps on the PC do not represent the Quest".
9. Screenshot attached; the report describes in words whether the pieces can be told apart (blocks or models) and whether the preset looks "furnished".
10. **Block fallback (run 4, step 9)**: line `[soglia] debug failmodels=1: every furniture model fails to load`; line `[soglia] furniture models loaded=0 fallback=14`; **14** `warn` lines `[soglia] furniture model unavailable <id>: using a block` (one per catalog entry with a model: they are expected and are **not** errors); line `[soglia] furnish applied style=scandinavian pieces=14 invalid=0`; exactly **14** `furniture:*` entities (the same ids as check 1), all `valid`, with `ui:reason-*` absent; sofa `z` ≤ 3.19 and bookcase `x` ≤ 10.70 as in check 1 (the footprints of the blocks coincide with those of the models); **0** `error` entries, 0 `Missing glyph info`, 0 `budget exceeded`; `callsPerView` ≤ 100 and `triangles` ≤ 150,000 on the last `[soglia:stats]` line (with blocks expected lower than the models case: the report gives the numbers); `shadowCasters` = 0. Screenshot attached (blocks recognizable by size and color). If `loaded` ≠ 0 or `fallback` ≠ 14: FAIL (the parameter was not read: check the `params source=` line and that `debug=1` is present).

## Outcome
PASS if checks 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 are met for runs 1 and 2 **and for the six measurements of step 4**, and check 10 for run 4 · FAIL otherwise. **Gate rule (user, 2026-10-06)**: only errors in console on the normal path, failed tests/build, violations of the `CLAUDE.md` rules and **> 100 draw calls per view** block; everything else (> 70 draw calls, menu cost > 12, triangles above the internal target of 60,000 but below 150,000) is a **WARNING**: it does not cause a FAIL, but it is reported and goes into `qa/device/DEBT.md` as "measure on the Quest". An M-worst measurement without a visible label is not valid and counts neither as PASS nor as FAIL: it must be repeated.
