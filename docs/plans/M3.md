**D29 · Minimal persistence = first slice of M4, not a separate system.**
- **What is saved**: `serialize(state)` (same version 1 format already tested) in `localStorage`, **one key per house**: `soglia:v1:state:<houseId>`. The `soglia:v1:` prefix belongs to the whole project: M4 adds other keys to it (issue reports) without changing the module.
- **What is restored** (whitelist in `pickRestorable`, pure logic): `furniture`, `nextInstance`, `history`, `prefs.onboardingStep`, `prefs.menuOpened`, `view` (from T3.10 on, only if the viewpoint exists in the house; otherwise `tabletop`). **Not** restored: `selectedRoomId` (the label would not exist: inconsistent state), `miniature` (scale, rotation, offset: D3 repositions the scale model at every session; declared decision, to be reviewed in M4), `role` (decided by the URL; in M4 it enters the list), `houseId` (it is in the key). If `deserialize` fails or `houseId` does not match: the data is discarded (`swarn`), the key is removed, and we start empty.
- **`reset=1`** deletes **all** the `soglia:v1:*` keys before reading and saving continues normally in the session. `furnish=scandinavian` **wins** over the saved data (the preset replaces the pieces, no history). An unknown `catalogId` in the saved data causes that piece to be discarded with `swarn`; the same applies to a `roomId` that is not a room of the open house (data saved before a change to the JSON). **Clarification from T3.1a (2026-10-10)**: an **empty** `roomId` (`''`: piece outside every room, produced only by furnishing presets) is **kept**; only a non-empty `roomId` that is not in the house is discarded. The restore happens after the catalog and **before** creating the scale model, onboarding and pieces (so onboarding and hints start with the restored preferences); without the catalog nothing is restored or saved (a valid saved state is not overwritten with an empty one); with `furnish=` only the preferences are restored (pieces, `nextInstance` and history from the saved data are discarded). Saving also happens at every `prefs` change. The saved data does not depend on `miniature` and `selectedRoomId` (`serializeForSave` writes them at the default values: moving the scale model causes no writes). The discarded history follows its pieces (entries with discarded pieces are removed, `nextInstance` stays). Size: 40 pieces + 20 history steps stay under ~200 KB, far below the 5 MB of `localStorage`; the log writes `bytes=`.
- **When it is saved**: at every `furniture`/`prefs` change with a 500 ms wait (coalesces), immediately on `world.visibilityState` ≠ `Visible`, `document.visibilitychange` → `hidden`, `pagehide` and end of session. The piece in hand is **not in the store** (a piece from the menu does not exist yet; one from the scale model is at the old pose until released), so **a half-way pose is never saved**.
- **Safe access** (`src/data/storage.ts`): every `localStorage.getItem/setItem/removeItem/key` behind `typeof window !== 'undefined'`, access to the `window.localStorage` property **inside** the `try` (it can throw `SecurityError`), write/read test once; if it is missing or throws: `swarn('feature localStorage unavailable; state not saved')` **once** and the app works without saving; quota full → `swarn('state save failed reason=<name>')` once, no exception. IndexedDB only if the quota becomes a problem (not before M4).
- **What M4 reuses**: `storage.ts` (safe access), `persistence.ts` (key naming, `pickRestorable`, save planner), the store subscriber, the lifecycle of D30. M4 only adds fields to the whitelist (`role`, issue reports), the version bump if the shape changes and possibly IndexedDB.

**D30 · Lifecycle: hidden session and resume (expected behavior, acceptance criterion).**
- **Sources** (all with feature detection): `world.visibilityState` (IWSDK), `document.visibilitychange`/`pagehide`, end of session (already handled by `PinchInputSystem`). On Quest, taking off the headset gives `hidden`; the system menu gives `visible-blurred`; the page may also be frozen or the session may end: all three cases lead to the same set of actions.
- **On `hidden` or `visible-blurred` (`suspend`)**, in this order: (1) **immediate save**; (2) **clean cancellation of the grab** (the same `cancel` function as M2, reason `suspend`): a **piece from the menu** goes back to the catalog (the `nextInstance` counter is not consumed: no skipped id, no duplicate); a **piece from the scale model** goes back **to the pose it had at the grab** (no `moveFurniture`, no history entry); the preview frame (`ui:furniture-preview`) is disposed; (3) two-hand gestures and `pan` end with the current values (`miniature gesture end` / `pan end`); (4) the palm menu closes and the detectors reset; (5) the pinch flags go to false and the claims are released (`pinchClaims.endSession()`): without this a lost `selectend` leaves the hand "pinched" forever.
- **On `visible` (`resume`)**: nothing is reopened on its own; the menu again requires the palm up (0.4 s) and the 300 ms guard starts (no residual pinch opens the menu); the scale model is not repositioned (D3 applies only at session start).
- **Discarded choice**: "place the piece at the last valid pose". It would change the arrangement while the user is not looking and would pollute undo; coming back is predictable and costs only a new grab from the menu.
- **Verifiable criteria**: number of `furniture:*` entities = number of pieces in the store before and after (no duplicate, no loss); pose of the pieces already placed unchanged; 0 `ui:furniture-preview` entities; after the resume a new grab works and the piece from the menu has **the same id** as the cancelled one; 0 `error`.
- **Provable in IWER only in part**: no MCP tool changes `visibilityState`. We prove (a) `xr_end_session`/`xr_accept_session` (real end/start of session), (b) **a development hook** with `debug=1`: key `F8` = hidden, `F9` = visible (`browser_interact` `press`; no combination with `Shift`; if the keys do not reach the page, `keyDown`/`keyUp`, and as a last fallback a debug entry in the `ui:glyph-test`-style panel behind `debug=1`), which go through **the same `applyVisibility` function** that the subscription to `world.visibilityState` calls (to be verified in T3.1b whether the Signal can be written instead, which is more faithful); QA declares that the hook **simulates** the event and does not prove that the Quest emits it. The real event and `document.visibilitychange` remain **headset only**.

**D31 · Palm up and pinch point from the joints (WebXR Hand Input).**
- **Primary source**: `inputSource.hand` (feature detection) and `XRFrame.fillPoses` (one call, preallocated `Float32Array` array, **no allocation per frame**; `getJointPose` allocates an `XRJointPose` per call) on the joints `wrist`, `index-finger-metacarpal`, `pinky-finger-metacarpal`, `thumb-tip`, `index-finger-tip`, in the reference space of `renderer.xr`. `gripSpace` remains **only a fallback** (hand not tracked, `hand` absent, controller, `null` pose).
- **Palm**: normal = `−Y` of the `wrist` joint (WebXR convention: +Y comes out of the back of the hand) **or** cross product (index − wrist) × (pinky − wrist) with the sign per hand: the spike chooses which one matches IWER's `Q_UP` (the cross product depends only on three positions and not on orientation conventions: preferred if they agree). The threshold (35°/55°, 0.4 s/0.25 s, 300 ms guard) does not change.
- **Pinch**: the **state** stays that of the `selectstart/selectend` events (it is the system's recognizer, it is the one the user knows and the one that drives IWER's `xr_select` tools); the pinch **point** becomes the **midpoint between `thumb-tip` and `index-finger-tip`**; the distance between the tips serves a detector with hysteresis (`< 0.02 m` start, `> 0.03 m` end) kept in pure logic as a **fallback and diagnostic**, to be enabled only if the headset shows that the `select` events are not enough (decision in M6). **Declared interpretation**: "pinch computed from the joints" = point and distance; the state does not change source.
- **Development parameter** `pinch=auto|grip|joints` (default `auto` = joints if present): `grip` restores the M2 behavior to retry the existing scenarios with their constants (`pose = target − (−0.038; −0.003; +0.037)`). With `joints` the emulator constants **change**: the spike measures them (T3.2a) and hands them to `emulator-qa` (memory `reference_hand_pose_sequences`).
- **How it is read** (verified on the type declarations on 2026-10-09): `world.onXRFrame((frame) => …)` gives the live frame; `frame.fillPoses(spaces, referenceSpace, transforms)` wants an `XRSpace[]` array and a `Float32Array` of 16 floats per joint: **both preallocated** per hand (5 joints → 80 floats), and it returns `false` if a joint has no pose (then the sample is "not tracked" and the grip is used). The sample writes the positions (translation column) and, for the wrist, the full matrix; no object per frame.
- **Delivered in T3.2b (2026-10-10)**: palm normal = −Y of the wrist (the cross product is only a consistency check: beyond 60° of deviation the palm uses the grip). **Discovery**: in IWER `selectstart` arrives with the fingers still open (tips at 0.094 m; closed, 0.002 m, one frame later), so the **announcement** of the pinch (log `pinch <hand> start`, listener) waits for the tips at ≤ 0.03 m, at most 0.15 s (`PINCH_POINT_MAX_WAIT_SECONDS`, `isPinchPointFinal`); with `pinch=grip`, untracked joints or tips already closed it is immediate. The **state** remains driven by the `select` events. For QA in IWER with `pinch=auto`: `xr select` with `duration` ≥ 0.4 s (or `set-select-value` 1 + 1.5 s). On Quest, if the system recognizer fires with the tips already close, the wait never kicks in (to be tried: `DEBT.md`). New and old constants in the memory `emulator-qa/reference_m3_hand_constants.md`.
- **Proof of the fallback in IWER**: `xr_set_connected` on the right hand (disconnection) → `input source=grip hand=right reason=no-joints` and the menu and the pinch keep working with the grip; reconnection → back to `source=joints`. If `xr_set_connected` does not remove the `inputSource`, `pinch=grip` tries the same path.
- **Declared fallback of the spike**: if in 45 minutes the joints cannot be read in IWER, the code stays written, tested with synthetic hands and **feature-detected**; the app uses the `gripSpace` as today; entry in `DEBT.md` ("joints not exercised in the emulator").

**D34 · FitCheck: rules and codes.**
- **Graph**: nodes = rooms + `outside`; edges = `door` openings with `connects`; route = fewest doors from `outside` (`entrance` door) to the destination room (the room that contains the center of the piece, `roomAt`); `no-route` if none exists.
- **Furniture** (`kind: furniture`, measures `w × d × h`, sorted `a ≤ b ≤ c`): passes through a door if `a ≤ width + 0.01` **and** `b ≤ height` (it is tilted); otherwise, if `disassemblable`, outcome `fits-disassembled`, otherwise `blocked`. **Mobility** (`kind: mobility`): `size[0] + 2 × 0.05 ≤ width` with numeric tolerance 1e-9 (no centimeter of tolerance). **Corridors not checked** (the JSON has no corridor data; in the demo houses the corridor of A is 1.6 m wide and would not change any outcome): the text declares "Simplified check"; `docs/DATA_FORMATS.md` is aligned in T3.7.
- **Codes distinct from D16**: the FitCheck result uses `door-too-narrow`, `door-too-low`, `fits-disassembled`, `no-route`; it **never reuses `blocks-door`**, which is the 0.30 m free zone in front of the door (D16) and remains an invalid placement reason.
- **Messages** (English, in `strings.ts`; whole cm): `Won't fit: the door is 80 cm wide, the sofa's shortest side is 85 cm` (short name: lowercase, without the initial "My "); mobility: `Won't fit: the door is 75 cm wide, the wheelchair needs 80 cm`; disassemblable: `Fits when disassembled: the door is 90 cm wide, the bed's shortest side is 95 cm`; low: `Won't fit: the door is 210 cm high, the sofa needs 230 cm`; passes: `Fits: the narrowest door on the way is 90 cm wide`; `No route from the entrance to this room`. Fixed second line `Simplified check`.
- **Expected outcomes on the real data** (computed by hand, 2026-10-07): `my-sofa` (0.85 / 0.95 / 2.3) in **A → blocks `door:d-living` (0.80)** on the route `d-entrance` (0.90, passes) → `d-living`; in **B it passes** (all 0.90, entrance included); in **A in the entrance hall (`hall`) it passes** (only `d-entrance`). `my-bed` (0.95 / 1.6 / 2.0, disassemblable) toward **any room** of A or B → `fits-disassembled` on **`door:d-entrance` (0.90)**, because 0.95 > 0.90 + 0.01 and the door that blocks is the **first of the route** (correction of 2026-10-09: the 07/10 version said `d-bedroom` 0.80, but `d-entrance` comes first and already blocks; `hall` included, because the route is only `d-entrance`). `my-desk` (0.70 / 0.75 / 1.4) passes everywhere. `wheelchair` 0.7 → 0.80 needed: **A bathroom blocks `d-bathroom` (0.75)**, A living room/bedroom/study passes (0.80 ≤ 0.80), **B bathroom passes** (0.80). `stroller` 0.6 → 0.70: passes even through the bathroom of A. **`sofa-3seat` from the catalog (0.85) does NOT pass through `d-living` of A** (D26 i): consistent for a "already present" furnishing; the preset never shows a FitCheck label until the piece is taken.
- **When it is shown**: the outcome is **derived** (not saved) for every piece; it is **shown** for the piece in hand (after 200 ms of stable state, like D27) and for the **last placed piece** for 8 s (`FIT_LABEL_SECONDS`, injectable clock), with a `ui:fit-label` label (only one, reused), a **red** marker on the blocking door and **green** markers on the doors of the route that pass (`ui:fit-marker-<doorId>`, up to 4, **a single instanced mesh**, see D33). Mobility pieces (`wheelchair`, `stroller`) are **normal pieces** (collisions, `MAX_PIECES` cap): the FitCheck depends only on the destination room.

**Tool**: the line `[soglia:stats:groups] house=.. furniture=.. hands=.. ui=.. markers=.. other=..` (only `debug=1`, every 2 s) estimates the calls by **scene traversal** (meshes visible along the chain, excluding zero instances and hidden materials, without the frustum). At 0.05 the sum coincides with `callsPerView` in all the measured cases; at 0.12 it exceeds it by 5 (pieces outside the frustum): the sum is an **upper bound** apart from culling. Classification (`src/logic/draw-groups.ts`): `house|room|wall|door|window|fixture:*` and `table:plinth` → `house`; `furniture:*` → `furniture`; `ui:*` → `ui`, **except** `ui:fit-marker-*` → `markers` (the FitCheck markers sit in the house and will be instanced together with the viewpoints by R-B); `viewpoint:*` and `pin:*` → `markers`; everything under `world.player` (hand models) → `hands`; unknown names → `other` and **inherit the group of the parent** (the meshes under an id have no name). `other=1` is a nameless mesh at the scene root, at the origin (from the framework; origin not verified). `ui:ghost-hand-*` and `ui:furniture-preview` count as `ui`. Rule for the following tasks: every new **permanent** element writes the cost in calls per view in the commit message and updates this table; the tape measure markers (T3.14) must be added to `MARKER_UI_PREFIXES`.

**Reductions, from the cheapest (cost in agent hours)**
1. **R-C · single-call fallback block** (−1 per block, −4…−14): 0.3 h, **in T3.4**.

With R-A: M3 80 → 64, M4 84 → 68, M5 95 → 79 (high ~90).
4. Not worthwhile: reducing the hands (they are seen, rule 1), removing the icons (D25), cutting `MAX_PIECES` to 24 (worsens the experience).
