**Milestone**: M2 · **Type**: PC (IWER emulator via MCP `iwsdk-runtime`)
Implementation prerequisites: T2.3, T2.10a/b, T2.11, T2.12 (bar with icons and `ui:menu-recenter`), T2.16 (hint, for check 11), T2.17b (Recenter action, proven in S2.9). See `docs/plans/M2.md`, decisions D18, D25, D28.

## Preconditions
- `dev-params.local.txt` = `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1`; `browser_reload_page`; `xr_accept_session`; `xr_set_input_mode` with `mode: "hand"`; wait 2 s. The head stays at (0; 1.6; 0). O = (Ox; Oy; Oz) from the last `miniature placed` line (expected (0; 1.35; −0.45)).
- **`Q_UP`** = quaternion of the `gripSpace` with the palm facing up, **fixed by the T2.11 spike** and written in `docs/plans/M2.md`, section "Spike outcomes". **The scenario does not run until `Q_UP` is written.** For the first attempt of the spike: the IWER identity orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)` probably has the palm down (to be checked); candidates for palm up: 180° rotation around the hand's z axis `(0, 0, 1, 0)` or around the x axis `(1, 0, 0, 0)`. `Q_UP_RIGHT` = the same quaternion mirrored for the right hand, if the spike requires it.
- Hand positions: `L_MENU` = (−0.25; 1.15; −0.20) (below the plane of the miniature, at ~0.55 m from the head); `R_MENU` = (0.25; 1.15; −0.20). "Resting" hands (no menu): `(∓0.30; 1.10; 0.10)` with orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)`.
- Common conversions and sequences: "QA strategy" section of `docs/plans/M2.md`. Every `xr_set_transform` **always** passes an explicit `orientation`.
- **Menu rule, opening and closing** (constants in `src/logic/menu-thresholds.ts`, M2 gate correction; D18 updated): the palm must stay up for **at least 0.4 s** before opening (`PALM_OPEN_HOLD_SECONDS`, no longer 0.25 s); the menu **does not open** if the **same hand** is pinching, if **any hand** is holding a piece, if a **two-hand gesture** or the **`pan`** is active, **nor within 300 ms** after the end of one of these (`MENU_RELEASE_GUARD_SECONDS`). **These conditions prevent ONLY opening: they never close an already open menu.** **The only closing** is the **palm beyond 55° for 0.25 s** (palm down, as in step 6). With a piece in hand the already open menu stays open but **dimmed and not selectable**. **Structure**: the menu is **ONE single `ui:palm-menu` panel**, updated **in place** on page change (not recreated); the `ui:menu-*` entities (items, Undo, Back, Next, Recenter) are **light anchors** (`Transform`, `Visibility`, `StableId`, `MenuItem`, **without `PanelUI`**) in the same positions as before, used as pinch targets and as reading points. So an opening after a pinch/release arrives **no sooner than 0.3 + 0.4 = 0.7 s** after the end line (one-frame tolerance: ≥ 0.68 s is accepted). All the waits of this scenario are ≥ 0.9 s after the palm pose; time is read from the timestamps (`ts`) of `browser_get_console_logs`. **The head is not rotated** (no `xr_look_at` before step 4): the head's forward direction stays (0, 0, −1) for the cone calculations.
- The pinch produces the lines `[soglia] pinch left start` / `pinch left end` (and `right`): they are the time base of steps 9a–9d.

## Steps

Note the index of the `ui:palm-menu` entity (needed by check 4). `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "menu|catalog"` (includes the line `[soglia] menu view maxAngleDeg=… distance=…`, one per opening). **Central cone**: with the head at (0; 1.6; 0) and facing (0, 0, −1), for `ui:palm-menu` and for each item compute the angle `acos(−dz / |d|)` with `d` = position − (0; 1.6; 0). From the command line: `grep -n "menu.undo\|menu.next\|menu.previous\|menu.recenter" src/ui/strings.ts` (the button texts exist).
4. `browser_screenshot` → attach (menu open, at the left below the miniature, items readable; the Undo, Back, Next, Recenter buttons show **icon and text**).
5. Pages: `xr_set_transform` `hand-right` at the position of `ui:menu-page-next` (orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)`), `xr_select {device: "hand-right", duration: 0.2}`; wait 0.5 s; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:menu-item-` and with `^ui:palm-menu$` (same entity index as step 3: the panel updates in place); log. 6. Closing: `xr_set_transform` `hand-left` at `L_MENU` with orientation `(0, 0, 0, 1)` (palm down); wait 1 s; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:(palm-menu|menu-)`; log.
7. Right: `xr_set_transform` `hand-right` at `R_MENU` with `Q_UP_RIGHT` (or `Q_UP` if they coincide); wait 0.9 s; `ecs_find_entities` with `^ui:palm-menu$`; log; then palm down and wait 1 s.

   b. **Pinch of the other hand (does not block)**: `hand-right` at rest `(0.30; 1.10; 0.10)` with `xr_set_select_value` `hand-right` = 1 (pinch in the air, far from the miniature), wait 0.3 s; `hand-left` at `L_MENU` with `Q_UP`; wait 0.9 s; readings (T_b1). `xr_set_select_value` `hand-right` = 0; wait 0.5 s; readings (T_b2).
