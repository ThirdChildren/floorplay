## Conventions
- **Hands**: buttons and controls are read with `ecs_query_entity` `["Transform"]`. The fixed buttons are three planes per side, centered at (±0.4347; 1.495 `Menu` / 1.565 `Issues` / 1.635 `View`; −0.5665); `View` and `Issues` at 0.715 m and 37.6° from the head. **IWER calibration (T5.7)**: to pinch at point `T` with the `joints` source (`pinch=auto`) the hand is set to `T + (0; 0.0245; 0.012)`; the log `[soglia:hands] … event=pinch-start point=` gives the real point. It is needed for the **`−`/`+` rows of the View panel** (3.3 cm); for the 8 × 6 cm buttons `pinch=grip` with `T − D_GRIP` (M4) is also enough. **This scenario runs with `pinch=auto` and the calibration**; `xr_select {duration: 0.3}`. Editing `.uikitml` files or sources during the session reloads the page and loses the XR session.
- **Reference table** (goldens of the T5.1 test; Turin 45.07° N 7.69° E; **local** time of the house; azimuth tolerance ±1.5°, height ±1°):

| `sunTime` | UTC | azimuth (from north, clockwise) | height |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-12-21T10:00 | 09:00Z | 145.5° | 13.7° |
| 2026-12-21T15:00 | 14:00Z | 215.6° | 13.2° |
| 2026-06-21T14:00 | 12:00Z (daylight saving time, UTC+2) | 197.6° | 67.6° |
| 2026-06-21T15:00 | 13:00Z | 227.3° | 61.8° |
| 2026-12-21T22:00 | 21:00Z | – | < 0 (night) |

- **Golden areas of the patches** (m², ± 0.05): A living room 21 Dec 10:00 = **2.3029**; A study 21 Jun 18:00 = **1.1616**; B bedroom 21 Dec 12:00 = **2.4284**. A, 21 Dec 15:00: **no** patch. A, 21 Jun 14:00: living room **≈ 0.1** (< 0.15). Patches under 1 dm² are not drawn.

- **Where the sun disc falls** (house A, `northAngleDeg` = 225, up = −z has azimuth 135°): for an azimuth β the plan unit vector is `x = sin(β − 135°)`, `z = −cos(β − 135°)`; the disc sits at `center + radius × (x, z)` in house coordinates. Check: the angle between `(marker − center)` projected onto the plan and the expected unit vector is ≤ 5°.
- Windows of A on the north side of the plan (`z = 0`, outward normal azimuth 135°): `win-living-1`, `win-living-2`, `win-bedroom`, `win-study`; east side (`w-east`, azimuth 225°): `win-study-east`; west side (azimuth 45°): `win-living-west`, `win-bathroom`.

## Preconditions

## Steps
1. **At 10:00**: standard setup; log `pattern: "sun|state|view"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `^sun:` (→ `sun:dial`, `sun:marker`), `^light:` (→ `light:patches` plus the `light:win-*` anchors); `ecs_query_entity` on `sun:marker` and `miniature:root` with `["Transform"]`; `browser_screenshot` → attach (sun patches in the living room, sun disc on the ring, north notch).
2. **Open the "View" panel**: pinch on `ui:view-button-right`; `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:view-` (→ panel, close and the **four** rows `zoom`, `turn`, `month`, `hour`; `contrast` and `text` only after T5.11), `^ui:palm-menu$` and `^ui:issue-panel$` (→ 0); `ui_inspect` (header `Sun 14° SE`, values `100%`, the value of `Turn` **read** (not always `0°`: it is the absolute rotation of the model), `Dec`, `10:00`; all `fontSize` ≥ 2.4 cm); log `pattern: "view"`. Then, for the "one at a time" rule: pinch on `ui:menu-button-right` → the View panel closes by itself (`view panel closed via=menu` or `via=panel`: report the line) and the `pinned` menu opens; close the menu again and reopen the View panel.
3. **At 15:00**: press `ui:view-hour-more` 5 times (read the position at each press if the panel is rebuilt); log `pattern: "view step|sun"`; `ecs_find_entities` `^light:`; `browser_screenshot` → attach (**no** patch in the whole house: living room in shadow, low sun at 13°).
4. **Month and daylight saving time**: press `ui:view-month-more` 6 times (December → June, with the December → January wrap) and `ui:view-hour-less` 1 time (14:00): log `pattern: "sun set|sun light"`. Then `ui:view-hour-more` 4 times (18:00): log `pattern: "sun light"`.
5. **Night**: from 18:00 in June bring the hour to 00:00 (6 presses of `ui:view-hour-more`, with the 23 → 0 wrap): log `pattern: "sun"`; `ecs_find_entities` `^light:`; `browser_screenshot` → attach (no patch, disc absent or off).
6. **Persistence and precedence**: wait 1.5 s (`state saved … sun=…`); rewrite the file **without** `reset=1` and **without** `time=`, reload: log `pattern: "sun set"`; then rewrite **with** `time=2026-12-21T10:00` and reload: log `pattern: "sun set"`.
7. **Real scale**: enter `viewpoint:V1` with the right hand (S3.1/S4.6), wait 2.5 s; `ecs_find_entities` `^light:patches$` and `^ui:view-button-` (visible? see check 8); `browser_screenshot` → attach (from the living room at real size, the patches on the floor); return with `ui:tabletop-button`.
8. **Default**: rewrite the file **without** `time=` and with `reset=1`, reload: log `pattern: "sun set"`.
8a. **Invalid `time=`**: rewrite the file three times with `time=2026-12-21T10:00x` (code after the minutes), `time=2200-01-01T10:00` (year outside 1900–2100) and `time=2026-13-01T10:00` (nonexistent month); for each one reload and read `pattern: "param time|sun set"`.
8b. **House B**: rewrite the file with `house=apartment-b&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1&pinch=auto&time=2026-12-21T12:00`, reload: log `pattern: "sun set|sun light"`; `ecs_find_entities` `^light:`.
9. Unfiltered log (`error`, `uncaught`, `Cannot read`, `reading 'add'`, `Missing glyph`); cleanup: select 0 on both, hands at rest, `xr_end_session`, delete `dev-params.local.txt`.

## Checks (all mandatory)
1. **Step 1**: the line `[soglia] sun set time=2026-12-21T10:00 source=url utc=2026-12-21T09:00Z az=<a> el=<h>` with `a` = 145.5° ± 1.5 and `h` = 13.7° ± 1; `sun light windows=…` with at least **`win-living-1`** and **`win-living-2`** and `rooms=` containing `living:2.3` (the log writes the areas with **a single decimal**: read 2.2–2.4; the exact golden **2.3029 m²** is in the T5.3 test and can also be read, if needed, from the area of the patch polygon); the other lit windows (at gate A/B: `win-bedroom`, `win-study`, with `bedroom:1.2` and `study:1.0`) are reported in the report with their area (not golden); **no** `light:win-living-west`, `light:win-bathroom`, `light:win-study-east`; 1 `sun:dial`, 1 `sun:marker`, 1 `light:patches`; the sun disc within ≤ 5° of the expected direction (formula above); `miniature:root` unchanged compared with startup.
2. **Step 2**: panel open (1 `ui:view-panel`, 0 menu, 0 Issues panel); line `view panel view maxAngleDeg=<a> distance=<d>` with `a` ≤ 30.0 and `d` between 0.50 and 0.65; the values and the header of the `ui_inspect` as listed; no text in Italian.
3. **Step 3**: after 5 presses `view step row=hour value=15:00` (and the intermediate 11:00…14:00), `sun set time=2026-12-21T15:00 source=ui utc=2026-12-21T14:00Z az=215.6±1.5 el=13.2±1`; `sun light windows=- rooms=-`: **no** patch in A (the living room is in shadow: the spot falls in the next room and is clipped); **0** `light:win-*` entities; `light:patches` without visible geometry.
4. **Step 4**: after the wrap `sun set time=2026-06-21T14:00 … utc=2026-06-21T12:00Z az=197.6±1.5 el=67.6±1` (daylight saving time: the UTC offset is 2 h, not 1 h); the intermediate steps go through `Jan` … `Jun`, never beyond December of the year; at this hour the living room has **about 0.1 m² (< 0.15)** (`rooms=` with `living:0.1`: windows to the SE, reveal 0.25 m, strip of about 0.04 m, physically correct; the first draft said 0) and `bedroom:0.0`/`study:0.4` are allowed (with an area < 0.05 m² the room still appears in `rooms=`); at 18:00 `sun light` has `study:1.2` (golden 1.1616 m², one decimal in the log: read 1.1–1.3).
5. **Step 5**: at 00:00 (June) `sun below horizon time=2026-06-21T00:00 source=ui utc=2026-06-20T22:00Z`: the rule is "height < 0 ⇒ no patch (`windows=-`, `rooms=-`), sun disc hidden"; **`sun:marker` has `Visibility.isVisible` false** (correction after gate A/B: before it stayed true and only the disc was not drawn), while the **`sun:dial` ring stays visible** on the model at night; `light:patches` not visible; in the panel the header says `Sun below the horizon`; no NaN and no `error` line.
6. **Step 6**: without `time=`: `sun set time=<last value> source=saved`; with explicit `time=`: `sun set time=2026-12-21T10:00 source=url` (the explicit value wins over the saved one).
7. **Step 7** (with `time` such that there are patches: for this step use `time=2026-12-21T10:00` after step 6): at real scale `light:patches` exists and is visible (the patches on the floor in the screenshot) and `[soglia:stats:groups]` gives `sun=1`; the **dial** `sun:dial` and the disc `sun:marker` are hidden (`Visibility.isVisible` false for both: the marker is too at real scale after the gate A/B correction); the "View" panel does not open (the "View" buttons are not there, like "Menu" and "Issues", and the return is `ui:tabletop-button`).
7a. **Step 8a (invalid `time=`)**: in all three cases the line `param time="…" ignored` (warn; for the year `ignored: year must be between 1900 and 2100`), `time` is **not** explicit and the line `sun set … source=default` (or `saved`) has minutes `00`; no `error` line, no NaN in the log; the hour never becomes the one written in the invalid value.
7b. **Step 8b (house B)**: `sun light` has the bedroom with **2.4284 m²** (in the log `bedroom:2.4`, read 2.3–2.5); at gate A/B: `sun light windows=win-living,win-bedroom rooms=living:2.8,bedroom:2.4` with the entities `light:patches`, `light:win-living`, `light:win-bedroom`; report the other rooms.
8. **Step 8**: line `sun set time=<ISO> source=default` with minutes `00`; if `az`/`el` say height < 0 then the hour is **10:00** (default rule). The value depends on the real clock: the **format** is checked, not the value.
9. **Budget**: no `budget exceeded` line in the whole scenario.
10. No `error` entry; no `Missing glyph`; no `reading 'add'`; no network request (the sun is computed locally).
11. **Headset only** (medium): azimuth and height plausible compared with the real sun at the given hour; patches legible at 1:20; "simplified light" understood.

## Outcome
PASS if checks 1–10 (and 7a, 7b) are met · FAIL otherwise. The azimuth, height and area values are the goldens of the unit tests of T5.1 and T5.3 (comparison with an independent implementation of the USNO formulas, not with the NOAA calculator: no network); a value outside tolerance is a FAIL.
