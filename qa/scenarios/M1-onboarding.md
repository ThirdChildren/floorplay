# S1.6 · Onboarding with the ghost hand (no text)

> **Changelog 2026-11-06 (M5 gate, F-C2-1; not an app defect)**: since M5, right after `onboarding step=done`, the coach of the "Menu" button (T5.15) creates `ui:ghost-hand-right` and `ui:coach-menu-button` at the same moment as `coach step=menu-button reason=start`; the onboarding hand (`ui:ghost-hand-left`) disappears. Checks **4, 9 and 10** no longer ask for "0 `^ui:ghost-hand-` entities" after `done`: they ask for **0 `ui:ghost-hand-left`** and, for the right hand, that it exists only if `ui:coach-menu-button` also exists (i.e. it is the coach's hand, not a leftover onboarding one); if the coach is not active (saved with `coach` already `done`, or after its 20 s timeout) the search `^ui:ghost-hand-` must give **0**. Step 11 (9 s timeout) stays unchanged up to `step=done`. The coach's hands are not a defect: they are verified by `qa/scenarios/M5-menu-onboarding.md`.

**Milestone**: M1 · **Type**: PC (IWER emulator via MCP `iwsdk-runtime`)
Scenario added by the planner. Prerequisite: T1.13. If T1.13 slips to M2 (user's decision) this scenario is BLOCKED and outside the M1 gate.

## Preconditions
- Headless runtime; `dev-params.local.txt` = `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1`; `browser_reload_page`.
- Headset at (0; 1.2; 0); `xr_accept_session`; `xr_set_input_mode` `mode: "hand"`.
- The hands stay **away from the miniature** and with no pinch: `hand-left` at (−0.40; 1.40; 0.20), `hand-right` at (0.40; 1.40; 0.20), select at 0.
- **O** = origin read from the last `miniature placed` line.

## Steps
1. Right after `browser_reload_page` and **before** `xr_accept_session` (outside a session): `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "onboarding|miniature placed"` (only the lines of the current load: start from the line `params source=`) and `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^ui:ghost-hand-"` (must give 0: outside an XR session the ghost hands do not exist). Then `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode` `mode: "hand"`, `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:ghost-hand-` **immediately** (in the same sequence of calls, with no waits: best-effort attempt, see check 1b), wait 4 s and repeat `browser_get_console_logs` with the same `pattern`. The hands stay away and with no pinch (preconditions).
2. `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^ui:ghost-hand-"` (at ≥ 1.5 s from the session start, i.e. after the wait of step 1).
3. `browser_screenshot` → attach (ghost hand above the miniature).
4. First pinch: `hand-right` at (Ox; Oy + 0.10; Oz + 0.10), `xr_select` with `device: "hand-right"`, `duration: 0.2`. Wait 1 s. `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "onboarding"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^ui:ghost-hand-"`.
5. `browser_screenshot` → attach (two ghost hands).
6. Two-hand gesture: phase A of `qa/scenarios/M1-two-hands.md` (steps 1–5, hands at ±0.15 m → ±0.25 m, then release). Wait 1 s. `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "onboarding"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^ui:ghost-hand-"`.
7. `browser_reload_page` (same parameters), `xr_accept_session`, wait 4 s. `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "onboarding"`.
8. Check on the strings, from the command line: `grep -n -i "onboard\|ghost\|hand" src/ui/strings.ts`.
9. `browser_get_console_logs` with no filter (`error`).
10. **One-hand exit: room selection.** `browser_reload_page` (same parameters), `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode` `mode: "hand"`, hands away as in the preconditions, wait 2 s. First pinch not on a room as in step 4 (`hand-right` at (Ox; Oy + 0.10; Oz + 0.10), `xr_select` `duration: 0.2`): wait 1 s and check `onboarding step=two-hands`. Then select the room `living` as in `qa/scenarios/M1-room-label.md` step 1 (`hand-right` at (Ox − 0.145; Oy + 0.25; Oz − 0.065 + 0.02), `xr_look_at` towards (Ox − 0.145; Oy; Oz − 0.065), `xr_select` `duration: 0.3`). Wait 1 s. `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "onboarding|room selected"`; `ecs_find_entities` with `namePattern: "^ui:ghost-hand-"`. No two-hand gesture in this step.
11. **One-hand exit: timeout.** `browser_reload_page` (same parameters), `xr_accept_session`, `xr_set_input_mode` `mode: "hand"`, hands away, wait 2 s. First pinch not on a room as in step 4; wait 1 s; `browser_get_console_logs` with `pattern: "onboarding"` (note the timestamp T0 of the line `step=two-hands`); `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:ghost-hand-`. Then **no action** (no pinch, no `xr_select`, no gesture, no room): wait a total of 4 s from T0, `ecs_find_entities` `^ui:ghost-hand-`, `browser_get_console_logs` `pattern: "onboarding"`; wait up to 11 s from T0 and repeat the logs and `ecs_find_entities`.

## Checks (all required)
1. Order in the logs after the reload (step 1), for the current load:
   a. Before the session **one single** `[soglia] onboarding step=pinch` appears, right after the **first** `[soglia] miniature placed ...` (the one outside the session, `y=0.950`, created when the world is created; observed 3–6 ms later; the criterion is the order, not the delay). `[soglia] house loaded ...` may appear in between.
   b. After `xr_accept_session` a second `[soglia] miniature placed ...` appears (session start, `y=1.350` with the head at 1.6 m) and `onboarding step=pinch` **does not repeat**: after the session the count of the `onboarding step=pinch` lines of the load is still **1**, and no other `onboarding` line appears until the user acts.
   c. `ui:ghost-hand-right` **does not exist** before the session (search of step 1 outside the session: 0 results). It does exist at the search of step 2, at ≥ 1.5 s from the session start (1 entity). The ghost hand only appears in an XR session, after **1.0 s of stillness** (`SHOW_DELAY_S`) from the first frame with the head pose: so **not** before 1.0 s from the session start. The search right after `xr_accept_session` is informative only: if it already returns 1 entity and the report can prove (timestamps of the session start log lines) that less than 1.0 s has passed, it is FAIL; if the elapsed time cannot be proven, note "not measurable" without FAIL. The exact 1.0 s timing is covered by the unit tests of `src/logic/onboarding.ts` (`shouldShowHint`) and by the headset test.
2. Step 2: exactly 1 entity, `ui:ghost-hand-right`; **no** `ui:ghost-hand-left`.
3. Step 4: `[soglia] onboarding step=two-hands` appears; now `ui:ghost-hand-left` **and** `ui:ghost-hand-right` exist (2 entities).
4. Step 6: `[soglia] onboarding step=done` appears; the search `^ui:ghost-hand-` returns **0 `ui:ghost-hand-left`** and, at most, one `ui:ghost-hand-right` **only if `ui:coach-menu-button` also exists** (the coach's hand; correction 2026-11-06, M5): without an active coach, **0** `ui:ghost-hand-*` entities.
5. Step 7: after the reload the sequence starts again (`step=pinch`): in M1 there is no persistence (it arrives in M4; this check must be inverted in S4.4 when `prefs.onboardingStep` is saved).
6. Step 8: no line found that contains onboarding text (the onboarding has no text); any matches are only function/key names and not visible phrases.
7. No `error` entry in the console. No controller used (`xr_set_input_mode` stays `hand`).
8. The screenshots show (description in words in the report): the ghost hand above the miniature, then two hands; **no text** overlaid.
9. Step 10 (one-hand exit, room selection): after `onboarding step=two-hands` (first pinch) 2 `ui:ghost-hand-*` entities exist; after the selection of `living` `[soglia] room selected living area=23.9` and `[soglia] onboarding step=done` appear (the order between the two lines is not fixed, no two-hand gesture was made); the search `^ui:ghost-hand-` returns **0 `ui:ghost-hand-left`** and a `ui:ghost-hand-right` only if `ui:coach-menu-button` is also there (coach's hand; correction 2026-11-06, M5), otherwise **0** entities.
10. Step 11 (one-hand exit, timeout): after `step=two-hands` (T0) 2 `ui:ghost-hand-*` entities exist and at 4 s from T0 there is **not** yet `step=done`; with no action `[soglia] onboarding step=done` appears at T0 + 9.0 s ± 0.5 s (9.0 s = 3 repetitions × 3.0 s of `REPEAT_PERIOD_S`; observed 781.45 → 790.45) and at 11 s from T0 the search `^ui:ghost-hand-` returns **0 `ui:ghost-hand-left`** and a `ui:ghost-hand-right` only as the coach's hand (with `ui:coach-menu-button`; correction 2026-11-06, M5), otherwise **0** entities. No `room selected` line in this step.

## Result
PASS if checks 1–7, 9 and 10 are met · FAIL otherwise.
