**S3.0 check 4**: the scenario asks for `state none` after `reset=1` (and no `state restored` line). With the report seed (M4, T4.2) every load saves the state 0.5 s after start, so the next load logs `state restored pieces=0`. The intent (the reset really deletes; 0 pieces restored) is met. **Scenario not modified** (user's rule); proposal: accept `state restored pieces=0` as a valid result.
